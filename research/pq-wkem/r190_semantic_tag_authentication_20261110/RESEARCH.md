## Exact claim restricted to the pinned CheckContextualAuxPowTag loop
The loop processes each unspendable parent-coinbase output, finds the first pchSyscoinHeader marker M, searches anywhere AFTER that marker for the expected bytes E = serialized branch ancestor hash and uint32 reference height, and counts marker-bearing OUTPUTS rather than occurrences. It does not require E to be immediately adjacent and does not reject a second M inside the same output. Thus the local check admits, for any unequal 36-byte Y, an output with bytes OP_RETURN || M || Y || M || E, assuming the other contextual inputs are valid. It also admits OP_RETURN || M || arbitrary bytes || E. Two distinct marker-bearing outputs are rejected under that loop. An independent full consensus check might impose other rules, so this is not a claim about network acceptance or current Core head.

## Why this is security-relevant to the ORIGINAL challenge relation
A naive bridge parser that reads the first 36 bytes after M would treat Y as an authenticated tag even though the pinned contextual verifier accepted E corresponding to the candidate child history. A false fork claim could then be formed from just one unaltered pure child (or two parent-work certificates of the same child). ## Constructive semantic normalization and limited proof
For a fully authenticated candidate child branch B at child height n in the applicable Nexus regime, set h=10*floor((n-6)/10) and define O(P)=(h, GetAncestor(B,h).hash). The parent coinbase tag is checked against this computed E, under Core-compatible scan semantics. O(P) is derived from the branch, not from any first-occurrence marker substring. For a fixed authenticated child history and height, this is a deterministic unique semantic value, independent of extra marker-looking bytes and different parent WorkIDs of the same child. Two qualified histories at the same referenced h with different ancestor hashes are incompatible. At distinct heights, authenticate the actual higher branch ancestry down to the lower height and compare. Do not infer incompatibility from incomplete omitted paths. This recipe must be applied under real Bitcoin parent/coinbase/Merkle, Syscoin header/height, interval/tag, source-window and LC-depth authentication; none can be supplied by a caller's Boolean flag.

A deliberate policy to veto outputs with multiple marker occurrences may be stricter than the pinned Core rule, but is a DIFFERENT explicit authenticated tag-fault predicate and has a liveness/griefing cost. A malformed-tag branch authenticates the outer carrier without first demanding its disputed tag check pass. A complete authenticated coinbase is needed for absence; an unrelated OP_RETURN payload is not a source fault.
