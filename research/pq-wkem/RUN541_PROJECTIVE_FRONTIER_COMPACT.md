NEW CANDIDATE: For public q-ary matrix columns a_i and target y, ORIGINAL witness w is binary of Hamming weight <=B with Aw=y. Honest classical PPT setup samples secret alpha_j and small errors e_ij per capability bit, publishes p_ij=<alpha_j,a_i>+e_ij mod q, and defines K_j=Round(<alpha_j,y>). Alpha is resampled if its y inner product lies within B*eta+1 of a rounding boundary. Every valid w obtains Round(sum_i p_ij*w_i)=K_j, since the error is <=B*eta. Distinct valid w can give different intermediate states but identical K. No canonical witness or common preimage is imposed. The full public view includes p and the public native checking-key correlation (a SHA-256 surrogate in the test).

FATAL CHOSEN-FALSE-STATEMENT ATTACK: Observe A, then choose y*=2a_0. Vector z=2e_0 satisfies Az=y* but is not a binary ORIGINAL witness. Public p reveals K_j=Round(2p_0j) because its error <=2eta<=B*eta. A terminal-level Booleanity check cannot revoke information already disclosed in p. For independent uniform A columns, every binary w obeys Pr[Aw=2a_0]=q^-n; a union bound gives Pr[there exists a valid w]<=sum_{r=0}^B binom(m,r)/q^n. Thus a chosen mathematically false statement exposes ALL signing-capability bits in classical polynomial time. The attack still works with honestly generated secret coins, independent capsules, deterministic hash expansion, erasure, public checking keys, and context separation. It directly defeats arbitrary-QPT false-instance hiding for THIS interface, but not general LWE, RIO, or hash-based WKEM.
