Identities are scoped to the exact statement/capsule and are never interpreted as public Boolean assignment labels.

## Why the restriction matters and why the remaining ORIGINAL bridge is NOT solved

A generic 3CNF clause over Boolean witness variables is NOT a credential OR clause: a dishonest source could present BOTH positive and negative freely available literal labels, even for x AND NOT x, and pass a monotone release despite no original satisfying assignment. Adding a single publicly fixed assignment hash/commitment would generally reject alternative valid original witnesses, violating every-ORIGINAL-witness correctness, and supplying only an inner proof is not an ORIGINAL extractor. R89/R98/R99 near-key/affine closure attacks, local switching, reversible frames, Boolean quotient off-domain evaluation and public-hash collapse remain mandatory regression tests. CNF cut synthesis is useful only AFTER a real source already provides unforgeable independently justified credentials. It cannot be substituted for the missing generic original-witness compiler.

The DNF contrast is also precise: for read-once P=OR_i(A_i AND B_i), each maximal unqualified set choosing one identity from each pair requires its unique complementary minimal honest blocking cut; 2^n such cuts are needed by the R107 single-guessed-cut technique. This does NOT prove impossibility of a different crypto compiler or reduction. It explains the actual efficiency gained for CNF and prevents claiming a universal polynomial cut theorem.
