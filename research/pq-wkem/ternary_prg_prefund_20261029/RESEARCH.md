## Exact real and simulated algorithms

Let G:{0,1}^kappa -> {0,1}^t be an independently assumed nonuniform-QPT-secure PRG with classical PPT evaluation, fresh independent seeds, and domain-separated independent positions. No particular implementation has certified 128-bit quantum strength here. For each vertex of the SOURCE 3COL graph, per independent round and capsule, the protected honest pre-funding verifier chooses two independent uniform t-bit strings sigma1,sigma2 and AUTHENTICATES them in the canonical CRS record before commitments. Bind this record to relation and statement, exact restricted SOURCE H=(A,b), claim/UTXO, path/branch, anchors/window, context, intended genuine native pk, unsigned base capsule CAP0 digest, all session/round identifiers, signing-key identities and permitted related-capsule identifiers. CAP0 is hashed before certificate generation, so the transcript has no circular digest dependency. The fully signed ceremony record is built afterwards.

For color c in {0,1,2}, set Delta(0)=0, Delta(1)=sigma1, Delta(2)=sigma2. With r sampled uniformly from kappa bits, publish ONE commitment C=G(r) XOR Delta(c). A valid opening reveals (c,r), requiring the exact equation and c in the three legal colors. The earlier two separate bit commitments per vertex instead published TWO t-bit commitments, with TWO independent CRS strings, and opened TWO seeds. The ternary scheme publishes ONE commitment under TWO CRS strings, opens ONE seed and has no invalid 11 code.

Witness-free simulator: for every vertex position independently draw r0,r1,r2 uniformly, publish sigma1=G(r0) XOR G(r1), sigma2=G(r0) XOR G(r2), C=G(r0). It authenticates the complete CRS before the first-message receipt and allows exactly ONE subsequent classical edge challenge. After the edge is selected, choose a uniformly random ordered pair of distinct colors for its endpoints and reveal r_c for each endpoint. Every such opening satisfies C=G(r_c) XOR Delta(c) EXACTLY. Unopened vertices' commitments remain opaque. A second edge on the same first-message vector must be rejected. All receipt, challenge, acceptance and final setup authentications are signed using the **actual** checking keys or explicitly permitted classical nonchallenge signing-oracle queries; never substitute forged native keys. Genuine native signatures on challenge-path sighashes are never provided at setup.

This procedure changes only the certificate attached to the existing honestly planted restricted TRUE-LWE SOURCE and its distributed same-native-key capsules. The SOURCE sampler, encryption, independent related-capsule coins, global N-1 corruption restriction, actual native checking-key correlation, and off-chain base-8 release equation remain as before. An arbitrary valid ORIGINAL witness still cannot compute the planted SOURCE opening.

## Proof: real-mode binding for even unbounded adversaries

Fix any deterministic G, and let D_G={G(u) XOR G(v): u,v in {0,1}^kappa}. Its cardinality is at most 2^(2*kappa). If C has two different legal-color openings, one of the three offsets sigma1, sigma2, or sigma1 XOR sigma2 must belong to D_G. In real mode each offset is uniformly distributed on t-bit strings (they need not be mutually independent), so by a union bound the bad-CRS probability per vertex is at most 3*2^(2*kappa-t), and for M vertex positions is at most

    beta_new <= 3*M*2^(2*kappa-t_new).

The predecessor has two independent bit commitments per vertex, so its corresponding global upper bound was beta_old <= 2*M*2^(2*kappa-t_old). Choosing t_new >= t_old+1 gives beta_new <= beta_old, without weakening this statistical guarantee. For illustrative, unvalidated kappa=256, M=2048, t_old=896, t_new=898: log2 beta_old <= -372 and log2 beta_new <= -373.4150375. These are NOT QPT pseudorandomness estimates.

For an actually FALSE restricted SOURCE, let its soundly compiled 3COL graph have E edges. Outside the bad-CRS event, every committed vertex has at most one legal opened color. If all E graph edges were answerable, they would form a genuine proper coloring, contradiction. When a protected honest pre-funding verifier signs the first-message receipt BEFORE choosing fresh uniform challenge edge, each round therefore succeeds with conditional probability <= (1-1/E) and k independent sequential rounds succeed with probability <= (1-1/E)^k. Add beta_new and QPT verifier-authentication forgery advantage for counterfeit records. For Q independently authorized complete attempts, union-bound by Q; unrestricted restart/grinding is unsafe. The K4 example E=6 has challenge error 2^-67.337 for k=256; k=487 is needed merely to lower that term below 2^-128. Very large general SOURCE graphs can make this impractical. Authenticated challenge ordering, fail-closed abort before irreversible funding, protected signer keys, no bypass path, graph verification and DA are real prerequisites, not conclusions of a signature on a retrospective transcript.

## Proof: straight-line one-open public-view QPT simulator

Condition on the disclosed seed r_c of ONE opened color. Replace the other TWO independent PRG outputs G(r_d) for d!=c by two independent uniforms using two ordinary nonuniform-QPT PRG hybrids. For c=0 the resulting C=G(r0), sigma1=G(r0) XOR U1 and sigma2=G(r0) XOR U2 are exactly honest real-mode distributions. For c=1, C=U0, sigma1=U0 XOR G(r1), sigma2=U0 XOR U2; the mapping from (U0,U2) to (sigma1,sigma2) is bijective and C=G(r1) XOR sigma1. The c=2 case is symmetric with C=G(r2) XOR sigma2. Hence the full single-opened (sigma1,sigma2,C,r_c) distribution after the PRG hybrids is EXACTLY honest real mode with independently uniform sigma1,sigma2, not merely matching marginals. Unopened vertices use three ordinary PRG replacements to make their (sigma1,sigma2,C) computationally independent uniform triples, hiding their real colors.

For any real valid 3COL witness, a fresh uniform permutation of the three colors makes the endpoint pair on each challenged edge uniform over the six distinct ordered pairs. The fake simulator chooses that pair after an adversarial, commitment-dependent SINGLE classical edge. Conservatively guess that edge and use standard PRG hybrids for all opened/unopened positions, giving polynomial comparison loss of order O(3*J*k*E*|V|*Adv_QPT-PRG), not a numerical QPT security level. All classical pubkeys, native/setup signatures, context, auxiliary data reproducible without secret SOURCE/native state, pre-funded related capsules and the finite global corruption model remain in the compared joint public distributions. No source-opening oracle, witness extraction, classical rewinding, quantum random-oracle programming, quantum-signing oracle, or multiple openings is assumed. It does not protect arbitrary hidden-secret-correlated quantum advice or active multiple-edge demands.

This is a certificate simulator only: honest-TRUE-LWE to uniform-LWE full-public hybrids still require the predecessor's independent decisional-QPT-LWE/leftover-hash/native-EUF assumptions and compatible honest-erasure setup. It is not an independently sound public NIZK for uniform FALSE H, a malicious-N-of-N CRS protocol, or any proof that recovery of a final native key yields an ORIGINAL witness.
