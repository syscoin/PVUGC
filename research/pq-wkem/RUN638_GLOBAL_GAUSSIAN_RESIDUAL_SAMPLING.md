## Construction and exact correction theorem
Factor the positive definite precision P=Q^{-1} as P=U^T diag(d_i)U, U unit upper triangular, d_i>0. For i=m,...,1, set s_i=d_i^(-1/2) and mu_i=-sum_(j>i) U_ij z_j; draw z_i from the scalar discrete Gaussian proportional to exp(-pi (z_i-mu_i)^2/s_i^2). Let Z_i(mu)=sum_(k in Z)exp(-pi(k-mu)^2/s_i^2), and W(z)=product_i Z_i(mu_i(z_>i)). This sequential law is exactly p_seq(z)=exp(-pi z^TPz)/W(z); do NOT silently assume the conditional normalizers independent of previously sampled coordinates.

By Poisson summation, Z_i(mu)=s_i[1+2 sum_(n>=1)exp(-pi s_i^2n^2)cos(2pi n mu)]. Define delta_i=2exp(-pi s_i^2)/(1-exp(-pi s_i^2)). Each LDL pivot satisfies d_i<=||P||op, hence s_i^2>=lambda_min(Q)>=s_F^2(1-alpha). If all delta_i<1, put C=product_i s_i(1+delta_i). Finally, accept the entire sequential sample z with probability W(z)/C. The accepted mass equals exp(-pi z^TPz)/C, so the conditional output is EXACTLY D_(Z^m,Q) in ideal real arithmetic. Acceptance is >=product_i(1-delta_i)/(1+delta_i). When s_F^2(1-alpha)=Omega(log(m lambda)) with sufficient security constant, this is 1-negl(lambda), independent of beta=tr(Theta).

If the final correction is omitted, write delta=max(delta_i)<1 and Rratio=((1+delta)/(1-delta))^m. The sequential law has TV distance <=min(1,(Rratio-1)/2) from the exact elliptic Gaussian. This follows by reweighting p_seq by W(z), whose maximum/minimum ratio is <=Rratio.

The scalar sampler can also be made explicit. For target f(k)=exp(-pi(k-mu)^2/s^2) with s>=1, draw a real Gaussian of width sqrt(2)s, round to integer k, and call its rounded-bin probability q(k). For any x in the bin, (x-mu)^2<=2(k-mu)^2+1/2, yielding f(k)<=M q(k), M=sqrt(2)s exp(pi/(4s^2)). Rejection with probability f(k)/(M q(k)) samples the exact scalar discrete Gaussian in ideal arithmetic; its acceptance is bounded below by (1-delta(s))/(sqrt(2)exp(pi/(4s^2)))>0 for s>=1. Numerical approximation of Gaussian draws, theta sums and accept/reject tests needs separately budgeted negligible delta_alg; exact real arithmetic is not a deployed implementation.
