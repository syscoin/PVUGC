For an n-bit target, decompose the public interval [0,T] into at most n disjoint aligned binary prefixes `P(T)`. Choose a private uniform 256-bit GGM root `s` and derive subtree keys `s_p` along each prefix with domain-separated SHA-256 expansions. Sample/freeze one exact 32-byte placeholder `sigma` and publish `E_p = sigma XOR SHA256("pad" || s_p)` for each p. The honest *proposed* Open checks the entire ORIGINAL source relation, finds the unique p matching Y(w), obtains s_p, and computes `E_p XOR SHA256("pad" || s_p)`.

Functional key convergence holds for every source matching a permitted prefix **only if** a permissionless opener can actually obtain the correct `s_p`. Constructing that delivery is the cryptographic heart of the problem.
