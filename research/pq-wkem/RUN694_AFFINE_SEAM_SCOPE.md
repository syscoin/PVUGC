**General interval invariant.** For a subgroup `H<=Sym(X)`, honest independent uniform frames `A_0,...,A_l in H` and public individually evaluable gates `G_i=A_i F_i A_(i-1)^(-1)`, every interval telescopes: `G_j ... G_i=A_j(F_j ... F_i)A_(i-1)^(-1)`. The interval double coset `H(F_j...F_i)H` is invariant. Different double cosets give disjoint joint supports; efficient exploitation requires an efficiently decidable invariant.

**New counterexample beyond Run 693's single-gate classifier.** On `F_2^3` set `H=AGL(3,2)` (1,344 frames). Let `T_A` flip bit 2 on `x0*x1=1`, and `T_B` flip bit 1 on `x0*x2=1`. Both are degree-two Toffoli involutions, affine-conjugate by swapping wires 1 and 2. Compare ordered underlying gate lists `Q0=(T_A,T_A,T_B,T_B)` and `Q1=(T_A,T_B,T_B,T_A)`. Both implement the identity. At EVERY position, the individual mixed-gate distributions under independent uniform affine left/right frames are exactly identical between worlds, because `T_A` and `T_B` lie in the same double coset.

Yet the first two gates have product `I` for Q0 and `T_B T_A` for Q1. Consequently publicly composing the two mixed gates yields an affine map in Q0 and a degree-two map in Q1 for EVERY choice of affine frames. The four-query binary second derivative `D_(u,v)P(x)=P(x)+P(x+u)+P(x+v)+P(x+u+v)` is always zero in Q0. In Q1, for independent uniform `u,v`, it is nonzero with exact probability `9/16` (two independent bilinear coordinates after invertible output recombination). After `t` independent trials false-negative probability is `(7/16)^t`; exhaustive 3-bit derivatives distinguish with certainty. This is a classical PPT attack and hence a QPT break of the restricted mixed-gate hiding experiment. It needs no ORIGINAL witness or setup secrets.

**Ideal positive comparison.** Uniform independent frames from the entire `Sym(X)`, with fixed endpoints, yield a uniform public factorization conditional on the total permutation, independently of its internal decomposition. This is exact but requires `Theta(n*2^n)` bits per uniformly sampled full permutation on `2^n` states. Compact public representations have no established QPT white-box security. Random reversible circuits' k-wise *oracle* pseudorandomness (He–O'Donnell arXiv:2404.14648) does not settle exposure of circuit descriptions; this counterexample is NOT an attack on CCMR RIO (ePrint 2024/006). Only primary abstracts/metadata, not full literature proofs, were checked this run.

**Threat model and exclusions.** Honest setup/evaluation are classical PPT for affine frames; adversary sees full public gate descriptions, can perform chosen classical inputs or coherent evaluation, and may be arbitrary QPT. No hardness assumption or quantum rewinding. Seam/cancellation (1), subgroup gauge (2), multi-gate statistical fingerprint (3), chosen-input oracle (5) all fail. A single gate's distribution passes this test. Related statements/capsules (4), malicious retained randomness (6), cross-UTXO replay (7), public native checking-key correlation (8), and special coherent quantum attacks (9) are NOT secured by this toy example. Honest independent coins already suffice for the break.
