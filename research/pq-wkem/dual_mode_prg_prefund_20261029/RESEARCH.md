## Exact classical-PPT commitment algorithms

Let G:{0,1}^kappa -> {0,1}^t be a standard independently assumed nonuniform-QPT-secure PRG on classical outputs. No concrete PRG parameters are validated. Assign a separate sigma_j of t bits to each vertex-color-bit position of every proof round and related session; do NOT reuse one sigma for different bits. For total M positions, choose t>=2*kappa+lambda+ceil(log2 M)+margin.

REAL CRS: An honestly operating, protected pre-funding CRS/verifier signer samples every sigma_j uniformly and AUTHENTICATES the complete length-delimited vector bound to H, original claim/UTXO, intended native pk*, branch, anchor/window, capsule digest, session identity and permitted verification policy BEFORE commitments. Commit a bit b with independent r uniform in {0,1}^kappa, C=G(r) XOR (b*sigma_j). An opening is (b,r); recomputation must equal C.

SIMULATOR CRS: For each position independently sample r0,r1 uniform. Publish sigma_j=G(r0) XOR G(r1), C_j=G(r0), retain r0,r1 privately. Then the EXACT SAME C_j opens to b=0 with r0 or b=1 with r1. The simulator thus has a concrete equivocation algorithm rather than an assumed SourceOpen oracle, a hash-only release trapdoor or a designated internal witness representation.

## Binding, QPT joint-view hiding, and one-opening proof

For fixed G define D_G={G(u) XOR G(v):u,v in {0,1}^kappa}; |D_G|<=2^(2*kappa). Real uniform sigma_j belongs to D_G with probability <=2^(2*kappa-t). Therefore for M independent real positions:

  Pr[any real C_j can have opposite-bit openings] <= M*2^(2*kappa-t).

Outside this bad-CRS event, bit binding is information-theoretic even for unbounded quantum adversaries, and does not depend on their running time or number of attempted opening computations. The simulated CRS is intentionally not binding and must NEVER be used for a real funded ceremony.

The fake unopened *joint pair* (sigma,C)=(G(r0) XOR G(r1),G(r0)) is QPT-indistinguishable from independent (U,U') using two ordinary PRG hybrids; real (U,G(r) XOR bU) is QPT-indistinguishable from (U,U') using one. More strongly, after ONE bit is actually opened:

  fake b=0: (G(r0) XOR G(r1),G(r0),r0)
         ~_QPT (U,G(r0),r0), which equals real b=0;

  fake b=1: (G(r0) XOR G(r1),G(r0),r1)
         ~_QPT (U,G(r1) XOR U,r1), which equals real b=1.

Each hybrid replaces ONLY G(unopened seed) by a uniform string; the publicly disclosed seed remains genuine and satisfies the exact opening equation. All public checking-key signatures over the CRS and transcript are generated by the actual keys (or permitted classical setup-signing oracles), so this comparison preserves their correlations under efficient QPT postprocessing.
