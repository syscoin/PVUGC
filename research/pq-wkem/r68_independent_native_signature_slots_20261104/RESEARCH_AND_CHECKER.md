## 1. Construction: one independently hidden signature per preauthorized key slot

Original R65/R66 release assumes one precomputed native signature concealed under one root. With an N-of-N native spending path, a single combined signature generated by an untrusted builder could leak before funding. To remove that cross-signer concentration **without threshold signing or common signing-key material**, define a fixed, immutable classical manifest V: claim/UTXO, ORIGINAL relation identity, scope, x, anchors/window, challenge branch, agreed full spending graph, precomputed funding outpoint and tx templates, exact native signing/sighash message *derivation rule*, ordered native keys vk_1..vk_N, uniform source matrix A, q/p/source parameters, admissibility and preauthorization policy. The spending graph must require **all** N independent native signatures on the challenge path and prohibit an EC-key-path or other bypass edge. Normal timeout payout is separate and need not prove absence of a fork. It does not restrict ownership after legitimate whole-UTXO settlement. The native m*(V) is DERIVED, not a self-referential field in V. No later encrypted capsule digest may be inserted into the precomputed funding transaction/signing message and create a txid cycle.

For signer j independently: generate a QPT-EUF native signature key pair (sk_j,vk_j), and pre-sign ONLY the exact claim/path-scoped native m*(V), obtaining sigma_j. Sample independent 256-bit K_j and independent ordinary-LWE source secrets z_{j,t} and errors e_{j,t}. Parse K_j into B=16 16-bit words mu_{j,t}. Reuse the single common uniformly sampled A from R66, and publish actual rounded ciphertext C_{j,t}=Round_{q->p}(A z_{j,t}+e_{j,t}+Delta mu_{j,t} e0 mod q). **Important:** A being uniform and independent of malicious choices is an explicit, uninstantiated setup premise; this note does NOT solve malicious multi-party A generation or selective abort.

From a single whole standard-LWE generator G_A0(K_j) derive (S_j,Kbind_j) jointly, as in R65. Publish Dsig_j=sigma_j XOR TreeG^d(S_j), CK_j=F_Kbind_j("CK",Base_j) and Aux_j=F_Kbind_j("AUX",CK_j,Base_j). Base_j binds V, A, j, vk_j, C_j, Dsig_j, source policy, context, related capsule scopes and permitted opening policy; it excludes CK/Aux to avoid cyclic hashing. Authentic pre-funding offchain manifest + data replicas pin the capsule bytes. No online committee or release authority is involved.

A valid ORIGINAL-SIS witness r is binary, r_0=1, r^T A=0 mod q. For every honest signer j, r^T C_{j,t}=Lambda mu_{j,t}+rounding/noise, where T=m((p/q)E+1/2), Lambda>2T and p-(2^16-1)Lambda>2T. Honest signing, Build and Open are classical PPT. Honest participant secrets K_h, sigma_h, sk_h and source coins must be protected before the funding commitment and erased as appropriate. ## 2. Exactly what the QPT proof gains and excludes

Consider one fixed honest index h and nonuniform arbitrary QPT adversary controlling N-1 signers. Its pre-setup quantum advice/auxiliary inputs are independent of h's coins. It may choose *arbitrary* malicious CK/Aux, adversarial capsules and public annotations after seeing honest capsule, including correlations chosen as functions of that public capsule, and may adaptively request permitted independently generated related openings. It cannot request the hidden honest target K_h/sigma_h, learn h's ephemeral coins, change fixed V/native graph, use a coherent quantum target-opening oracle, or obtain a signing-oracle response on the targeted m*. Any legitimate public opening of the honest sigma_h ends secrecy. This model does not handle arbitrary target-secret-correlated quantum advice or malicious control/rejection sampling of the uniform matrix A.

The existing R65 standard-LWE whole-output generator hybrid jointly replaces native pad AND Kbind/CK/Aux (R52 tree expansion); it does NOT assert independence of CK from a real pad.
