## 1. Serialized classical candidate

Let R_x(w) be the ORIGINAL bounded verifier, with all three OR grounds and actual authenticated/relevant evidence requirements unchanged. Assume its Boolean circuit as a formal input, not a claim to have instantiated actual Core/LC. Sample uniform trapdoor t, publish h=H_trap(t), form Q(w,u)=R_x(w) OR [H_trap(u)=h]. Introduce Tseitin assignment a for Q with its output asserted, then F(a) is a polynomial-size 3CNF. Every original witness supplies a full satisfying a by deterministic circuit evaluation, as does t. A satisfying a projects to an original certificate or a trapdoor preimage. All source checks are inside F; no honest-code post-filter is supposed to protect the key.

Use a Boolean formula for F (balanced conjunction of clauses, depth O(log |F|)). Construct a polynomial-length permutation branching program with public read indices v_i, two factors g_(i,0),g_(i,1) at each position, consistent accepting product alpha (a 5-cycle), rejecting product identity. For completeness, a literal is the two choices I and a prescribed 5-cycle, swapped for a negated literal. Negation of a subprogram with output in {I,beta} uses beta times its inverse (and a fixed conjugation if a different cycle is desired). AND uses P Q P^-1 Q^-1 for child programs whose accepting cycles have commutator a 5-cycle, followed by a fixed conjugation to the desired cycle; if either child rejects, the commutator is identity. Such cycles exist: on {1,2,3,4,5}, using right-to-left composition, (1 2 3 4 5) and (1 3 5 4 2) have commutator a 5-cycle (1 4 3 5 2). OR uses De Morgan. Inverses reverse the factors and invert each choice. Fixed factors can be represented as positions with identical alternatives. Recursion grows by a constant factor per level, hence polynomial for the balanced formula. No assumption of NC1 evaluation of the entire original source circuit on w: a includes its complete gate assignment.

Each repeated read of a variable is a separate occurrence. For successive occurrences i,j of each variable, add a dedicated disjoint cyclic marker of prime order p. The choice b at i contributes c^(b) and at j contributes c^(-b); all other factors contribute identity on that marker. Products are zero exponent iff bits agree (p>=3). Consequently the full marker product is identity iff every repeated read is consistent. Take r independent copies of the program on disjoint base supports; add markers also between their reads of the same variables if desired. The combined group is an explicit permutation group on d=5r+pE points, E the number of marker equalities. All marker supports are initially disjoint. It is one permutation after transport, not a tuple of separately encrypted keys. Each base copy may choose its own accepting cycle. Let A denote the permutation with accepting cycles on all r base supports and identity on all markers. Every satisfying assignment yields exactly A, independent of its internal bits and source branch. Setup can evaluate it using t.

For each position sample independent uniform rho_i in S_d (including endpoints), publish P_(i,b)=rho_(i-1)^-1 g_(i,b) rho_i as explicit permutations, plus read indices, x,h,ctx. ctx binds the entire claim, native VK/message, capsule ID, etc. The ciphertext mask is S XOR XOF(ctx,encode(rho_0^-1 A rho_L),|S|), where S is the exact frozen safe native capability. Build erases t and private factors/gauges. Open constructs a from the original certificate and multiplies the published factors for the bits of a. The telescoping product is rho_0^-1 A rho_L for every qualifying original witness. This is a serialized classical polynomial candidate, with actual same-output correctness; no ideal evaluator. It is NOT secure. Its polynomial degree can still be prohibitively large, and there is no claim of practical size. All security statements below concern explicit honest public capsules.

## 2. Public-template attack, including noncommuting supports

Let a fixed all-zero occurrence path define U_i=g_(1,0)...g_(i,0), V_i=P_(1,0)...P_(i,0), with U_0=V_0=I. Then V_i=rho_0^-1 U_i rho_i. For each position form the public normalized deviations

    D_i = U_(i-1) g_(i,1) g_(i,0)^-1 U_(i-1)^-1
    E_i = V_(i-1) P_(i,1) P_(i,0)^-1 V_(i-1)^-1.

Exactly E_i=rho_0^-1 D_i rho_0. For any occurrence sequence, multiplying its D_i for positions with bit 1 in increasing order, then U_L, gives its untransported terminal product. The analogous E_i product times V_L is its transported product. This identity follows by cancellation of consecutive zero-prefix products; it is noncommutative.

If g and A are reconstructible from the public template, the known T=A U_L^-1 belongs to the subgroup generated by D_i, because the setup trapdoor gives a path. Obtain a straight-line word for T in those generators by constructive membership in an explicit permutation group of degree d (stabilizer-chain membership). The standard polynomial-time permutation-group algorithm can retain straight-line expressions for its strong generators: orbit transversals are products of earlier generators, Schreier generators are products/inverses of transversals and generators, and sifting T expresses it as a product of transversals. Store expressions as a DAG rather than expanding potentially long words. Evaluate the same straight-line word using E_i in place of D_i, obtaining rho_0^-1 T rho_0, then multiply V_L to recover rho_0^-1 A rho_L exactly. No witness, source verification, or guessing of endpoint gauges. This uses the standard constructive permutation-group membership result, not an external lookup or a measured implementation.

Unlike component splicing, this attack does not require commuting markers or disjoint supports: it uses public-template normalization and any polynomial-degree explicit permutation representation, even one with genuinely overlapping supports. It does not establish a general attack against hidden-template programs. Do not spend more runs changing the public-template group, degree, marker prime, endpoint permutations, or hash placement.

## 3. Hidden-template repair: remove the public D_i, but retain independent marker action

The explicit mathematical change is to sample the child target cycles/conjugations privately, independently per replica, and omit g and A from the capsule. Open still works from read indices alone; public constructive membership above no longer knows T. To isolate this repair, first retain the public recursive parse and read-occurrence map (not private cycles). For each copy, the parse gives a relaxed accepting sequence: recurse through AND/OR/NOT with desired subformula truth values, choosing literal bits independently in each invocation, respecting inverses by reversing the selected sequence. For AND true both children must be true; for OR true choose a feasible child; for false use the corresponding truth cases. Feasibility when all literal occurrences are independent is computed bottom-up (constants and fixed factors included); a satisfying consistent assignment exists by t, so the root true case is feasible. Repeated copies of a child in a commutator receive the same desired child truth, which suffices for the same child output cycle, regardless of its actual private value. No original assignment is found. Use a separate relaxed sequence for every replica. Its base product is the hidden A's base part, while its marker product M can be nontrivial. This is a full occurrence word, not an adversary-provided witness.

Pick a publicly known consistent rejecting assignment a0 to F (e.g. violate a designated asserted acceptance-output bit; add a fresh asserted unit variable if necessary). Its base product and all marker products are identity. Let B be the published product on a0, and L_rel the published product on the relaxed sequence. Then

    B = rho_0^-1 rho_L
    L_rel B^-1 = rho_0^-1 A M rho_0.

For p=3, A^5=M^3=I and A,M commute. Hence (L_rel B^-1)^6 = rho_0^-1 A rho_0, even with arbitrarily many independent secret accepting cycles and a completely hidden permutation of the point labels. Multiplication by B gives the exact release permutation. This uses order separation, not knowledge of the factors, template, or witness. More generally any marker exponent coprime to five permits the same Chinese-remainder exponent trick. Hashing the entire permutation does not help.

Repair p=5 removes the order-separation attack. Screen separately, because it changes the mathematical premise of that attack. The normalized E_i in section 2 can still be computed from the public P alone. Their orbits on the d points are obtained by a graph search using each generator and inverse. On each orbit test whether every pair of E_i restrictions commutes. In the concrete disjoint-support construction, marker orbits are cyclic and commute. To ensure every base orbit is recognized as nonabelian, append to each base replica harmless pairs of occurrences of a variable, one pair implementing c^b,c^(-b) and one implementing e^b,e^(-b), with c,e noncommuting 5-cycles. Put them at the beginning of the replica before the ordinary program; consistent input cancels both pairs. On the all-zero reference path these pairs have identity base prefix, so their D_i include c,e (prior replicas act on disjoint base supports). They generate a transitive nonabelian action on that replica. Marker edges for these reads are included normally. This is a concrete trial with private target cycles, not a claim every possible branching program already has this property.

Consequently after global hidden conjugation the union of nonabelian orbits is exactly the union of base supports; marker points are in abelian orbits (or singleton orbits). Take the restriction of L_rel B^-1 to the nonabelian orbits and make it identity on all other orbits. This is a well-defined explicit permutation, equal to rho_0^-1 A rho_0. Then multiply B. The p=5 repair still loses exact S, on FALSE original instances as well. No independent hash/native break and no trapdoor preimage. Both attacks are deterministic classical polynomial operations on the public view; QPT security therefore fails before any simulator or composition is considered.

The public-parse requirement here is real. If one also hides the recursive program correspondence using private syntactic rewrites, the above relaxed-word construction is not justified merely by the public read sequence. A single width-five base copy still has only polynomially many possible conjugates of a single 5-cycle (<=d^5), so a native checking interface can test them; with many copies this enumeration can become exponential, and this observation alone is not an attack on the many-copy hidden-parse variant. A common relaxed word from a publicly shared template would restore the attack; independent hidden rewrites are a distinct unproved premise. Do not infer security from that gap. A proof would need to analyze all serialized permutation factors jointly, not merely count possible targets.

## 4. Noncommuting consistency repair and remaining operation

The direct-product markers make correct cancellation independent of intervening source operations, but also enable the attacks in section 3. A candidate repair is to put markers on overlapping support with base factors, so that no independent orbit projection or coprime-order filtering applies. For untransported base factors g_i(b_i), insert marker factors m_i(b_i) to their right. Write G_(>i)=g_(i+1)...g_L. Exact expansion gives

    product_i [g_i m_i] = (product_i g_i)
                         product_i [G_(>i)^-1 m_i G_(>i)],

with the second product in increasing index order. Even if m_i and m_j are inverse for equal bits, their conjugates by different source-dependent suffixes need not be inverse. Other markers can intervene as well. Thus merely overlapping the supports destroys the all-consistent-witness correctness proof. To force prescribed commuting transported marker contributions c_i(b_i) independent of the unknown source path, the actual local marker would have to be m_i=G_(>i)c_i G_(>i)^-1. A static two-choice table cannot in general encode that dependency, unless the corresponding conjugation is invariant over all feasible suffixes; that condition needs a proof and may restore removable central markers. Allowing a stateful public evaluator to compute suffix-dependent factors exposes its intermediate state and requires a new full-public analysis; it is not a supplied solution.
