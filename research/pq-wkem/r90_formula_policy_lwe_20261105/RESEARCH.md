## ORIGINAL relation and honest distribution

Unlike R89's insecure deterministic 3CNF-shaped LWE key, here the ORIGINAL relation is deliberately restricted to a polynomial-sized monotone AND/OR formula f over independently sampled, *real* LWE-public-key witnesses. For each leaf i, honest KeyGen samples A_i uniform, bounded error E_i from the declared vector-LWE distribution, and secret S_i; B_i=A_i S_i+E_i modulo q. The public statement x=(f,(A_i,B_i),scope) includes relation identity, claim/UTXO, branch, anchor/window, checking context, immutable graph and intended native verification key. An ORIGINAL witness is any subset of leaf secrets with valid short-error equalities whose leaf set satisfies f. Different valid witnesses may contain different subsets and distinct secret/error representations for each leaf; no canonical witness or common intermediate state.

All leaf keys are honestly and independently generated, with all source secrets unavailable to the adversary except an explicitly permitted unauthorized set U. Every such x is TRUE. This does NOT generate a FALSE-instance source or compile arbitrary ORIGINAL NP/SYS-BTC statements. A malicious builder who retains an authorized subset can open early, excluded here. An honest builder can see only x and the public keys; no leaf secret needed for release construction.

Each leaf uses the full public-key encryption source from R83, optionally packed as in R86–R88. Independent **ordinary decisional QPT-LWE for the exact declared key/error distribution**, together with the exact leftover-hash statistical bound for that PKE, implies chosen-message QPT IND-CPA. This is independently specified ordinary cryptography, not an assumption already guaranteeing common witness release. Honest setup and public source keys must have the same distribution used by that assumption. Honest algorithms are classical PPT; adversaries are arbitrary nonuniform QPT with independent quantum advice.

## Exact Build and Open algorithms

Let kappa be a declared PRG seed length. Choose T uniform in {0,1}^kappa. Assign it to the root of f. At each OR node, copy the parent share unchanged into both children. At each AND node independently sample kappa-bit Z, assign Z to the left child and parent XOR Z to the right child. Each leaf receives share t_i. Encrypt every leaf share independently as C_i=Enc(pk_i,t_i;r_i), using a **genuine independently random** leaf encryption, not an ideal source-opening oracle. Build needs neither an ORIGINAL witness nor any source secret. Share construction and evaluation are classical PPT in formula size and kappa.

Separately the honest N-of-N graph preauthorizes the frozen native message m0=Bind(relation,x,f,claim,UTXO,branch,anchors,window,graph,vk_native) and obtains its ordinary native signature sigma. Derive disjoint P||B=PRG(T), with P the full signature-length pad and B the checking seed. Publish Dsig=sigma XOR P and CK=F(B,Dsig,(C_i)_i,context,related_roots), Aux=G(B,Dsig,CK,(C_i)_i,context,related_roots), plus public independent preflight attestation. F and G are fixed classical PPT, acyclic, and cannot directly depend on T, P, sigma, leaf secrets or hidden challenge encryption randomness. No public sound proof requiring unknown encryption coins is claimed. Large evidence stays off chain.

To Open, the claimant supplies **actual ORIGINAL leaf secret/error witnesses** and their leaf indices; verify R_original off chain. Decrypt evidenced leaf ciphertexts using the supplied secrets. R83/R88's exact decryption-margin guarantee ensures all valid representations of the same pk recover its one fixed leaf share. Reconstruct at AND by XOR of both recovered child shares, at OR by either child; if both available they must agree. At a valid root obtain precisely T, compute P/B, recompute CK/Aux, unmask sigma and verify an ordinary native signature on the one exact intended message. Every satisfying ORIGINAL witness set reconstructs exactly the same precommitted T and sigma despite different intermediate states.

## Unconditional share privacy for an unauthorized fixed coalition

Per bit over GF(2), introduce basis coordinate e0 for T and one fresh independent mask coordinate per AND node. Each leaf has a public coefficient vector over these formal independent variables; OR duplicates vectors and AND splits a vector v into fresh e_j and v+e_j. For every leaf set U:

    f(U)=TRUE  if and only if  e0 is in the GF(2) row span of the leaf-share coefficient vectors in U.

By induction: at an OR, access to either child's parent share suffices; if both are unauthorized, their fresh independent subtree masks leave their *joint* views parent-independent. At an AND, at least one unauthorized child leaves the parent one-time padded by its independent sibling share. This gives perfect statistical independence between the **joint vector of opened shares** for any fixed unauthorized U and root T, not just independent marginal hiding. The exhaustive GF(2) checker verified the coefficient criterion for 275 full binary monotone formula shapes with 1..5 labeled leaves and all 7,882 coalitions, but this mathematical recurrence, not a toy test, is the proof.

## Genuine full-public joint QPT challenge and reduction

Fix U in advance with f(U)=FALSE. The adversary may know complete valid leaf secret keys, ciphertext plaintexts and checking metadata for all i in U; it may process the entire classical statement, all C_i, public native vk, Dsig, CK, Aux, context, independent attestations and independent related capsules coherently using arbitrary nonuniform QPT and independent quantum advice. Exclude target-root/target-signature disclosure, any authorized complete witness, secret-correlated auxiliary leakage, target encrypted leaf secret exposure, adaptive post-ciphertext selection of U, coherent decryption oracles, and malicious source key generation. Independently rooted sibling capsules with their own keys/seeds/messages and allowed openings are simulatable; correlated/shared target-root or opened target-key siblings are not proved.

H0 is the real full public view. H1 replaces each ciphertext C_i for i not in U by Enc(pk_i,0^kappa), one at a time. A reduction embeds one **actual ordinary QPT IND-CPA challenge** under pk_j using messages (t_j,0^kappa). It chooses T, every AND mask, other honest leaf keys and encryptions, and the native pre-signature, and constructs Dsig, CK, Aux and public preflight over the challenge ciphertext **jointly**. It never needs the embedded challenge secret key, encryption randomness, a supplied representation or a SourceOpen oracle. IND-CPA grants loss at most the sum over unopened leaves of their QPT PKE advantages. Public CK/Aux and attestation must be recomputed from the actual challenge ciphertext, including its intentional inconsistency with target T in the dummy world.

In H1, the unopened ciphertexts are independent encryptions of zeros; all disclosed secret keys and shares in U are jointly independent of T by the proven formula-sharing lemma. Replace PRG(T)=(P,B) by uniform disjoint (P',B') using the **independent QPT-secure classical-PPT PRG** of R85 (conditional on its exact ordinary-QPT-LWE assumption and parameter/statistical accounting), rebuilding every CK/Aux/attestation. This is a full-public straight-line hybrid, not a marginal pad argument. In H2, Dsig is uniform independently of the target signing secret. For exact T recovery, target success is 2^-kappa in H2. For producing any accepted native signature on the preauthorized target message, substitute a real independent QPT-EUF-CMA challenger, **without requesting a target-message signature**; unrelated authorized signing queries are allowed.

For any arbitrary QPT A against this precisely restricted distribution and fixed unauthorized U:

    Pr[A recovers exact T]
      <= sum_{i notin U} Adv_QPT-INDCPA-PKE_i + Adv_QPT-PRG + 2^-kappa.

    Pr[A outputs accepted native target signature]
      <= sum_{i notin U} Adv_QPT-INDCPA-PKE_i + Adv_QPT-PRG
         + Adv_QPT-EUF-CMA-native.

All public checking-key correlations and independent auxiliary records are simulated in the hybrids. No quantum rewinding, native forgery extraction from an adversary-volunteered representation, MinRank/SIS, RIO/ideal mixer or witness-encryption hardness assumption. QPT IND-CPA is independently instantiated from ordinary exact-distribution QPT LWE plus leftover hash for *honestly generated* leaf keys; this is not a generic NP reduction. A QPT adversary's ability to derive an unauthorized secret key and decrypt the unopened leaf would itself contradict the independently specified IND-CPA condition.
