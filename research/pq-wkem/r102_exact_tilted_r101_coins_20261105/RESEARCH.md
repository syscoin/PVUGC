## Explicit executable classical-PPT algorithm

The normalization Z=b^3+2 a b^2+2 a^2 b+2 a^3=25726. Sample each proposed coordinate independently using exact integer cumulative intervals from U uniform in {0,...,Z-1}: Pr[x]=W(x)/Z. Compute s=sum |r_i| and immediately reject r if s>2729. If s<=2729, independently sample integer V uniform in {0,...,b^{2729-s}-1}; accept iff V<a^{2729-s}. Repeat until accepted.
