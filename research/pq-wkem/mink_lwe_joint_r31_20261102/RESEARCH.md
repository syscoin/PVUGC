## Candidate A: ambient ordinary-LWE sample directly in the source
Attempted honest classical Build samples A uniform F_q^(m x n), s uniform nonzero F_q^n, independent bounded coefficient noise e and mask r, publishes C=B*r+A*s+e+Delta*mu*v. For ANY fixed valid ORIGINAL lambda_w, conditional on nonzero s, lambda_w A s is exactly uniform over F_q. Thus the intended witness computation lambda_w C is uniform, shifted by Delta*mu+lambda_w e. If correct decoding accepts L of the q residues, correctness probability is exactly L/q. For exact noiseless recovery it is 1/q, even for a witness known in advance. An ordinary-QPT-LWE assumption does not repair information-theoretic failure of the required same-capability correctness.

## Candidate B: repair witness correctness by restricting LWE to the relation span
Let A=B*U for uniform U. Now lambda_w A s=0 for all valid ORIGINAL witnesses. But A is distributed uniformly on V^n rather than ambient F_q^(m x n). Its exact statistical distance from the ordinary-LWE uniform A distribution is 1-q^(-(m-r)*n), so embedding the standard challenge directly would change the required joint distribution. Equivalently take T a public column basis of V and an ordinary r-row LWE pair (Abar,bbar=Abar*s+e_L), set A=T*Abar and C=B*r+T*bbar+e+Delta*mu*v. The public quotient satisfies the exact identity H C=H e+Delta*mu*H v. **All** embedded LWE challenge material, including e_L, disappears from this public projection. For every TRUE instance with a valid ORIGINAL witness, H v != 0 (otherwise v in V would imply lambda_w v=0). If source error e lies in V too, the public quotient directly reveals mu. For independent full-coordinate small e, secrecy of H e+Delta*mu H v is a separate structured-source noisy-syndrome problem, and cannot be inferred from ordinary-LWE indistinguishability of (Abar,bbar). A QPT distinguisher can compute H C coherently or classically. Publicly derived same-base related capsules do not remove this projection.

## Repairs tested and failed
Moving plaintext direction v into V to hide it from H also makes lambda_w v=0 for every valid witness, destroying completeness. Providing witness-specific correction of lambda_w A s at opening needs s or equivalent SOURCE-opening material, not derived from arbitrary ORIGINAL w. Subtracting A s in honest Build simply removes the LWE sample. These are failed alternatives, not a new WE or WPRF assumption.
