## Selection and target

Keep R_x(w) exactly the full original source relation, with the three independently sufficient OR branches, all authentication, relevance, window and maturity predicates and all claim/native bindings. No canonical witness assumption. S is the fixed safe cancellation contribution. The missing operation is public recovery of S using any such w even though Build does not know w. The last transcript-switch trial sought two responses at a fixed first message, and failed to transport hidden Q-side responses. This iteration instead seeks a *single identical complete proof byte string* generated by either an original witness or an independent honest setup witness, without using special soundness on two transcripts. It replaces reusable local labels and additive evaluators by a globally bound proof value; the local-label splice does not directly apply to recovering an exact full proof. The question is whether a public polynomial-time conditional collision sampler is constructible without already assuming the release.

## Serialized candidate

Use injective length-delimited encodings and independent hash domains. At security parameter lambda and fixed preassigned context ctx (including capsule ID, native VK/message and all claim bindings), Build samples t uniformly from {0,1}^lambda and computes y=H("setup",ctx,t). Define J_(x,y)(u) by the disjoint tagged OR:

    u=(0,w) and R_x(w)=1
    OR u=(1,t') and H("setup",ctx,t')=y.

Consider a noninteractive hash-only public proof generator P(J,u;r) and verifier V(J,pi); r denotes prover randomness. No existence of a suitable P with the properties below is assumed. Build samples its coins r0 and computes pi0=P(J,(1,t);r0). Define

    a = H("match",ctx,pi0)
    p = ExpandH("mask",ctx,pi0,|S|)
    c = S XOR p
    C = encode(ctx,x,y,a,c).

ExpandH has separate counter domains and public length. Build erases t, r0 and pi0. Open enumerates a polynomially bounded number q of candidate proofs pi_i=P(J,(0,w);r_i), tests V(J,pi_i) and H("match",ctx,pi_i)=a, and returns c XOR ExpandH("mask",ctx,pi_i,|S|) on a match; otherwise fail. Exact proof equality is sufficient for recovery; a match-domain collision is not sufficient for correctness. Native verification may additionally reject a wrong result but does not enforce original-source possession by itself.

This is an explicit algorithmic shell with missing P, not a constructed WKEM. It also does not yet provide malicious setup correctness. If P is a conventional randomized proof system, it is executable but generally fails completeness. The unusual necessary property is *conditional collision sampling*, for every w, of the setup-side proof distribution given the public capsule. A public low-entropy distribution alone would not suffice.

## Trial A: independent witness-indistinguishable samples

Fix x,y and condition on the public generation parameters, before publishing the proof digest. Let D_t and D_w be the proof-string distributions for the two witnesses. Ignore the match digest in the sampling step for this trial (it is only a filter). For q independent draws from D_w, exact success is

    sum_pi D_t(pi) [1-(1-D_w(pi))^q]
    <= q * sum_pi D_t(pi) D_w(pi)
    <= q * max_pi D_w(pi).

Even perfect equality of distributions does not imply nonnegligible collision probability. If D_t=D_w is uniform over 2^h strings, exact success is 1-(1-2^-h)^q. Thus statistical or computational WI alone is not a correctness argument. If the proof includes a fresh uniform lambda-bit salt in any fixed serialized position, the success bound is at most q/2^lambda (even ignoring all other differences). Publishing the salt and using it as common randomness only removes this one entropy term; it does not supply equality of proof contents.

An explicit repair is to use digest-dependent proof sampling rather than independent draws: given a and w, compute a valid pi with H("match",ctx,pi)=a. For a random independent match oracle and no way to identify pi0, searching a large proof space by arbitrary candidate trials does not make this efficient. This is not a general QROM lower bound for auxiliary-dependent search; the concrete missing algorithm must exploit a relation between the two proof generators. A proof generator coupled pointwise by public coins would be sufficient for correctness, but its availability cannot be inferred from WI. Do not assume it.

## Trial B: share-normalized global proof commitments

Try to instantiate the coupling, not just increase the number of samples. Use a public seed rho for normalized prover randomness and a hash-commitment-based proof of the disjoint tagged OR. As a concrete commitment layout for an MPC-style proof, encode u as a fixed-length vector including the branch bit, split it into three XOR shares s0,s1,s2 (s2=u XOR s0 XOR s1), and commit to each full share with

    d_i = H("share",ctx,i,s_i,nonce_i).

Use the same public derivation for rho and nonce_i in the two executions; derive s0,s1 from rho, changing only s2. This is a candidate coupling of the share prefix, not a claim that public MPC randomness preserves zero knowledge. A full proof must bind all three commitments before the hash-derived challenge and authenticate any required view openings and computation of J.

For setup u_t=(1,t) and opener u_w=(0,w), u_t != u_w even if their other bits coincide. Under the proposed normalization s2 differs, so equality of d2 between the two proofs implies a collision on different injectively encoded inputs. Even if the sampler changes all three shares and nonces rather than holding the first two fixed, equality of all three commitments for two different tagged witnesses entails a collision: if all three preimages are equal then XOR of their shares is equal, contradicting the different branch bit. This remains true when the share commitments are a prefix of a more elaborate proof and the hidden view is not opened. Thus in this concrete layout, a successful pointwise coupling of the full proof string across the two branches would produce a hash collision. Sharing coins repairs only the fresh-salt part of Trial A, not the witness-dependent commitment part.

Try a projection repair: match and mask on only the two normalized shares (or their commitments), leaving the branch-dependent third share and its commitment outside the KDF. This yields pointwise equality by construction. But for this normalization, those two shares and their salts are deterministic public functions of rho. Anyone computes their exact encoded projection, computes ExpandH on it, and recovers S, without either OR witness. If rho is kept private the opener lacks precisely those normalized values; publishing a proof of knowledge of rho is not an algorithm to derive them. If the projection also contains authenticated computation data that cannot be generated publicly, equality of the projection for both branches must be newly proved. The above shortcut then need not apply, but no such extra common hidden value is supplied by share normalization. In particular, validating the full proof before calculating the mask is an honest-code if-check that a caller can omit. Binding the mask to the entire proof instead returns to the commitment collision obstruction. A source-conditional opening tag on a public commitment authenticates arbitrary openings but does not make two different hidden full transcripts equal. It also cannot justify dropping binding from a proof knowledge extractor.

This is a specific failed coupling implementation, not a theorem excluding all hash-only proof systems or proofs with other commitment structures. No claim is made that every MPC-in-head proof contains these explicit share commitments; this was the layout selected for the attempted repair.

## Simulator and reduction obligations

The desired proof-based reduction has two distinct stages: recover an actual complete OR proof from arbitrary S recovery (or accepted native forgery), then extract an ORIGINAL w or setup hash preimage. Even supposing an ordinary proof of knowledge for J, J is always true due to t, including on false x. Ordinary soundness is useless. A proof extractor would need to work on proof recovery from the *correlated honest capsule*, not just from an independent prover. An adversary that recovers a pre-existing proof can replay it without necessarily possessing either witness. The standard proof-of-knowledge experiment cannot silently be substituted for this correlated recovery experiment.

In a classical independent random-oracle sketch, absent a query at pi0 in the mask domain, the unqueried mask is uniform and an independent pad is a plausible simulator. More exactly, if the adversary recovers S and has not learned its mask, either it already has independent information sufficient to produce S or it guessed the missing pad; the native checking key can test guesses, and its correlations must be included. If the adversary queries at pi0, a classical recording reduction can identify that query by the match hash (modulo collisions), but that gives pi0, not t or w. This sketch does not apply to arbitrary quantum coherent queries by observing an attacker query log. The QROM simulator would have to justify programming/recovering the hidden mask point, including the match-domain correlation, proof generation using the oracle, all native auxiliary correlations, sibling capsules and selective openings. It would also need a knowledge/extraction guarantee for a recovered proof under that very correlated view. No such joint simulator or extraction is constructed. Concrete SHA collision/preimage assumptions alone do not justify the sketch.

Even a hypothetical successful sampler needs a malicious setup procedure proving that the capsule holds the correct S and is openable by all original witnesses before irrevocable funding, with one honest contribution and no bypass. No change to the endpoint, slot-global versus activation-bound choice, offline N-of-N, source LC or network premises can fix a missing sampler. If the setup witness is retained by a dishonest contributor that contribution is not protected; honest contribution protection, graph binding and independent native-forgery handling still require proof.

## Handoff

The exact new equation for this approach is a *serialized* hash-only pair of algorithms for the two tagged witnesses, such that for every qualifying w,

    Pr_{Build coins, Open coins}[P_source(J,w,C)=pi0] >= 1-negl(lambda)

with pi0 produced without w, while the common proof remains hidden from unauthorized QPT attackers and arbitrary proof recovery from the correlated capsule yields an original certificate or independent justified break. A digest-filter on independent WI samples is not that algorithm. Deterministic public share normalization with globally binding commitments either prevents equality or, in the explicit projected layout, makes the mask public. An approach that merely postulates a collision sampler of this strength is a reformulation of the missing primitive, not independent foundation.
