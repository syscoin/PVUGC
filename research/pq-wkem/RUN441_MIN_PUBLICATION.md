Theorem: Let H be finite, T0,T1 in H, and U,V independent uniform secret endpoint frames. For two individually acyclic public capsules G0=V T0 U^-1 and G1=V T1 U^-1, each gate G_j separately is uniform H. Yet the attacker reads D=G0^-1 G1=U(T0^-1 T1)U^-1. The complete joint distribution depends exactly on the conjugacy class C of T0^-1 T1: G0 is independent uniform H and D is uniform C. There are |H||C| possible pairs, each with |Z_H(T0^-1 T1)| frame preimages. Different classes have disjoint supports. Proof: condition on G0=g; every U uniquely determines V=g U T0^-1, and conjugation by uniform U is uniform over C. This creates an overlay cycle even when each capsule has no internal cycle. Independent intermediate frames in multi-edge paths telescope out.

Sharp repair in this bare-table model: refreshing EITHER endpoint frame independently in the second capsule makes the joint two-gate distribution uniform on H x H, independent of T0,T1. This theorem DOES NOT include public input/output pins, decoders, checking-key correlations, extra related capsules, or malformed setup. Independent masks across contexts are necessary against this particular leakage but not sufficient for WE-like source-enforced release.

Efficient nonlinear example: H=(S3)^r. Embed each S3 factor as a nonlinear reversible three-bit gate permuting 000,001,010 and fixing the other five Boolean states. Source T0 is identity; T1 on factor i is identity for hidden bit 0 or a three-cycle for bit 1. The public relative conjugacy class recovers every bit with O(r) group operations even though either capsule alone has uniform bare gates. Exhaustive S3 counts: reused input AND output frames give disjoint supports 6 (bit 0) versus 12 (bit 1); refreshing either endpoint makes both supports all 36 pairs.
