**red(F)=K red(P_x)**,
since every ideal-mask term reduces to zero. Find the first nonzero coefficient c of red(P_x) by scanning its explicit coefficient vector and output
**K = coefficient_c(red(F)) / coefficient_c(red(P_x)) in F_p**.
This is a deterministic classical polynomial-time computation **in the size of the published full coefficient representation**.
