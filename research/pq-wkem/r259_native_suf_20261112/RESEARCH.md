## Independently specified hash-only SUF endpoint example
Lamport one-time signing for one fixed M: choose independent uniform s_{j,0},s_{j,1} in {0,1}^n; publish pk_{j,b}=H(s_{j,b}); set d=H_msg(M) and Sig(M)=(s_{j,d_j})_j. Verify exactly n canonically encoded n-byte preimages.

For arbitrary QPT A outputting a CLASSICAL alternate signature:
- Same digest with different signature bytes supplies some j with a second preimage s'_j != s_{j,d_j} but H(s'_j)=H(s_{j,d_j}). Embed a random second-preimage challenge at index j, losing at most n.
- Different digest supplies a changed bit j opening previously unrevealed pk_{j,1-d_j}; embed a random hash-inversion challenge in that unrevealed slot, losing at most n. Here M is frozen before key generation.
- Different messages with identical H_msg digests supply a message-hash collision.

Thus one-time Lamport strong unforgeability has a straight-line QPT final-output reduction to independent QPT one-way, second-preimage and collision resistance, with polynomial indexing loss.
