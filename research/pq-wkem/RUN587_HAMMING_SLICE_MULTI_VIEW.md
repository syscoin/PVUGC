# Run 587 — Hamming-slice false-key hiding versus correlated multi-capsule release

Date: 2026-10-17. Repository: syscoin/PVUGC#1; exact parent a3a9c27b10a176b0b3ca5743f1a3f97d7b27490c. This is a bounded extension/falsifier of the *rejected* Run 586 Regev-style restricted-language diagnostic, not a renewed claim of practical witness-KEM security.

Fix an odd prime q, m >= 2, B=1, A=1_m in F_q^(m x 1), H=(q-1)/2, and ORIGINAL R((A,b),(s,e))=[s in {0,1}, e in {-1,0,1}^m, b=As+e mod q]. For public b, a classical setup chooses secret mu in {0,1} and r uniformly in {0,1}^m, and outputs u=sum(r), v=b^T r + H mu mod q. If m<(q-1)/4, every valid ORIGINAL witness independently decodes the same mu by nearest-neighbor rounding v-su=e^Tr+H mu, for every r. Different witnesses have different intermediate residuals.

**Theorem A (strictly average-case one-bit hiding).** Draw b uniformly from F_q^m and publish (b,u,v), excluding a native public signing/checking key and correlated advice. Conditional on u=k, r is uniform on binom(m,k) bit vectors. For uniform b, h_b(r)=b^Tr mod q is pairwise universal; its expected TV distance from uniform F_q is at most 0.5 sqrt((q-1)/binom(m,k)). Therefore the bit-conditioned complete public distributions have TV at most

eps(m,q)=sum_{k=0}^m binom(m,k)/2^m * min(1,sqrt((q-1)/binom(m,k))).

The ORIGINAL statement is true with probability at most p=2(3/q)^m. Conditioning on a false statement increases the bound to at most eps/(1-p), since the conditioning event depends only on public b. For m=48,q=257, eps=3.963443729703789e-6, p<=3.3578423504683612e-93, and exact all-witness rounding holds. For m=1024 and prime 2^128<q<2^129, the conservative formula has log2(eps)<-444.33; this is idealized **average-instance** information theory, NOT arbitrary-false-instance, 128-bit implementation, or native-public-key QPT security. Independent auxiliary advice and quantum postprocessing cannot improve discrimination of the stipulated public bytes beyond TV. Correlated quantum advice/native PK.root can invalidate this conclusion.

**Theorem B (exact multi-capsule reuse falsifier).** Fix ANY b* (including an ORIGINAL-false one) and reuse its per-bit r in m different auxiliary capsules b^(i)=e_i. Each auxiliary statement is ORIGINAL-true with *two* valid witnesses: s=0,e=e_i and s=1,e=e_i-1_m. Encrypt independent bits mu_i; the published auxiliary ciphertexts satisfy v_i=r_i+H mu_i. Four residues {0,1,H,H+1} are distinct for q>=5, so an attacker reads every r_i and mu_i from v_i alone, then computes H mu*=v*-b*^T r. Hence full recovery of the target false-statement bit is deterministic, requiring neither source witness nor quantum computation. Repeating per bit with independently sampled r_j within each bit slot, but reused r_j across the claims, recovers an entire 128-bit capability. Different contexts, keys, branch identifiers and UTXOs do not stop the reused-coin leakage. Fresh independent r per capsule *prevents this particular attack*, but does not solve the Run586 selected-false-key attack.

The run587_slice_multiview_check.js included here is the EXACT executed Node-compatible checker and the accompanying JSON is its captured output. Neither checker proves cryptographic security. The finite examples with q <= 7 exercise the hash-distance theorem only, not the correctness threshold.
