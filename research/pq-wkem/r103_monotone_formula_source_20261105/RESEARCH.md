## Perfect joint unqualified-view privacy lemma

For any unqualified set U of credential IDs with P(U)=0, let Shares_U(t) denote the FULL JOINT vector of every plaintext occurrence share whose identity belongs to U, including all repeated-ID occurrences. For any t,t' in GF(256),

    Shares_U(t) is distributed IDENTICALLY to Shares_U(t').

Proof by induction: absent leaf gives empty view. At OR, U is unqualified in both branches, so each branch view is input-independent by induction; subtree randomness is independent, yielding joint independence even though both branches receive t. At AND, choose u uniform, v=t-u. At least one child is unqualified. If left qualified and right unqualified, the right view is independent of v, while the entire left view depends only on independent uniform u, hence the pair is independent of t. The symmetric case and both-unqualified case follow likewise by conditioning and independence. This treats repeated credential IDs as one set U that exposes ALL matching occurrences. Independent sharing tapes for each root byte lift exact privacy to the full T_j. Any qualified U reconstructs t by the recursive AND/OR algebra on every tape. No canonical witness or common internal state is required.
