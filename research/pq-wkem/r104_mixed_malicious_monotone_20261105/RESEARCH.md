## Proved joint-secret-sharing lemma, including repeated identities

Fix any C with P(C)=false. Define Shares_C(t) as the **entire vector** of plaintext shares of ALL leaf occurrences owned by C (not just the marginals) generated on independent uniform formula-sharing tapes. For all t,t' in GF(256), Shares_C(t) has exactly the same joint distribution as Shares_C(t').

Proof by structural induction. A missing leaf has empty view. At an OR, C is unqualified in BOTH children; their views are input-independent by induction, and independent sharing tapes give joint input independence even with repeated IDs. At AND, at least one child is unqualified; its entire view is input-independent by induction. The other child receives a uniformly random input u or t-u, independent of t; integrating over u gives full joint independence. Formula descendants retain independent randomness. All authorized paths reconstruct t exactly by AND addition and OR forwarding, with no need for identical intermediate states. Independent byte tapes lift the statement to κ-bit T. Corrupt ciphertexts may reveal every C share and every malicious encryption coin without changing the lemma if C's setup/coins are independent of the root.

Example P=(H AND (A OR B)) OR (J AND (A OR C)), C={A,B,C}. Neither H nor J is mandatory: {H,A} and {J,C} independently qualify; C does not. For two AND masks u,v over GF(5), the full corrupt vector (A_left,B,A_right,C)=(t-u,t-u,t-v,t-v) has exactly 25 equally likely possible patterns, identical for every t, despite the repeated A identity.
