## Exact joint-classical-public-view coupling lemma, not underlying security

This conditional distribution is **independent** of the particular e, mu or side information: the same uniform fiber applies. Arbitrary coherent QPT processing AFTER receiving the classically sampled transcript cannot distinguish these distributions, since their joint density operators are identical.
