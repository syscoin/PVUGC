Concrete monotone formula:
P = (H1 AND (H2 OR M)) OR (H2 AND H3).
M is an arbitrarily malicious/publicly decryptable source identity. H1,H2,H3 are independently honest LWE source keys. M alone cannot authorize. Any final corruption set containing M that remains unqualified is exactly {M}, {M,H2}, or {M,H3}. Each has at least one disjoint blocking cut. A uniformly secret cut guess therefore has success probability at least 1/2 on every eligible winning execution. Different authorized witness sets, notably {H1,M} and {H2,H3}, may retain different secret states and reconstruct different paths but get the SAME precommitted capability. This does NOT require an H1 credential for every valid witness.

## Straight-line full-joint-public QPT reduction

Let I_j be the target credential identities and let a small, explicitly efficiently enumerable family F_j of honest-only blocking cuts H satisfy both:
  (1) P_j(I_j \ H)=false for every H in F_j.
  (2) for EVERY eligible final unqualified C_j, some H in F_j is disjoint from C_j.
For the actual example F_j={ {H1,H2}, {H1,H3} }, K_j=|F_j|=2. No polynomial-size cut cover is promised for arbitrary succinct formulas: in general the family can be exponential, in which case this loss does not establish a polynomial-time reduction.

To analyze a fixed final target j, the simulator uniformly guesses H from F_j BEFORE public setup, but keeps this guess private. It embeds genuine ordinary IND-CPA challenge keys under exactly the honest identities in H. It generates all honest keys OUTSIDE H using fresh real S,E conditioned on the SAME challenger A, and all other related-capsule honest keys independently conditioned on A. It runs the genuine arbitrary QPT malicious-key generator over all actual public B, retaining the generator's quantum register. Thus all chosen-malicious-source/public-checking correlations are simulated together. All OFF-TARGET Reveal(k) answers are genuine. Any target Corrupt(j,i) with i outside H returns the REAL independently generated source secret, plaintext occurrences and coins; a query on i in H aborts. No secret challenge key, artificial SourceOpen oracle, quantum rewinding or adversary-supplied representation is requested.

Hybrid the ciphertexts of each protected H identity's leaf occurrence groups from the REAL shares to ZERO using genuine chosen-message ordinary nonuniform-QPT-LWE-PKE IND-CPA challenger ciphertexts. The simulator knows both candidate shares, does not need challenge source secrets, and always recomputes Dsig/J/CK/Aux, related records and any simulator-compatible audit field from the ACTUAL resulting ciphertext bytes. Every non-H target ciphertext remains real and may be opened after publication.

Because H is a blocking cut, P_j(I_j\H)=false. The R103/R104 AND/OR secret-sharing induction proves that the complete JOINT VECTOR of ALL non-H plaintext leaf occurrences—including repeats of H2 and malicious M—is perfectly independent of T_j, even if all those plaintexts, receipts and secrets are disclosed. The honest-H zero ciphertexts cannot leak T_j. Therefore the remaining public ciphertexts, all malformed-key leakage, all future permitted target corruptions, all off-target complete revelations and arbitrary QPT processing are jointly T_j-independent once the PRG is also swapped.

Use an actual QPT PRG challenge Y in place of PRG(T_j). The simulator can generate non-H shares against a dummy root because their entire joint law is root-independent, then compute the true Dsig/J/CK/Aux and related-capsule correlations from Y and actual challenged ciphertext bytes. In the final world:
rho_(T_j, entire_public_view, quantum_advice, adaptive_disclosures) =
   Uniform_kappa(T_j) tensor rho_(entire_public_view, quantum_advice, adaptive_disclosures),
on paths without protected-cut corruption. Hence arbitrary QPT exact-root output succeeds with probability at most 2^(-kappa).

For an ORIGINAL real winning event W_j, a private uniform cut guess avoids the final unqualified C_j with probability at least 1/K_j, so:
Pr_real[W_j] <= K_j * Pr_real[W_j AND (H intersect C_j is empty)].
This remains true with adaptively chosen C_j because the private guess does not change real public outputs before a forbidden query. Sequential genuine LWE-PKE/PRG full-joint hybrids change the bracketed event by at most Q_j*epsilon_PKE + epsilon_PRG + epsilon_binding,j, with Q_j the maximum number of challenged packed honest-H ciphertext groups. Pr[recover exact T_J from some eligible unrevealed target] <=
