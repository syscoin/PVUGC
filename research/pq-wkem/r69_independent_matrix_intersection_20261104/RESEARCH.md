## 1. Specific repaired obligation

## 4. Witness density, parameters and exact limitations

When **all** N matrices are honestly independently uniform, the concatenated A is uniform over Z_q^(m×Nn). Let X count binary r with r0=1 and r^T A=0. Then E[X]=2^(m-1)/q^(Nn). Distinct candidates r,r' have rank-two row constraints even over Z_(2^k): some coordinate differs, yielding a 2×2 minor ±1, hence their zero-kernel indicator variables are pairwise independent. Var(X)<=E[X], so by Chebyshev:

  Pr[X=0] <= q^(Nn)/2^(m-1).

This is an existence/liveness bound for **all honest matrices**, not an efficient witness-finding algorithm and not an SIS hardness theorem. It requires m larger than Nn log2(q)+1 by the desired statistical margin. Arbitrary malicious A_j may instead insert e0 as a column: then every r with r0=1 fails the relation and all-witness completeness is impossible; honest parties must not irreversibly fund on this unproven completeness. A corrupt party can also post a malformed ciphertext or hidden signature. Those malicious-liveness and audit obligations remain unproved. The scheme has O(Nmn log q) public matrix storage rather than O(mn log q) for R68's single matrix; the source-ciphertext byte count per signer stays as R66, and there is no numeric 128-bit QPT parameter claim. Toy dimensions q=65536,p=4096,m=8,n=2,N=4 are deliberately insecure and would not yield high-density all-honest instances; planted-matrix correctness tests are intentionally separate from genuine uniform challenge sampling.
