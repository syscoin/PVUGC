## Concrete source and release algorithm

R90–R96 require independent LWE leaf public keys. R100 samples ONE A uniformly in Z_q^(m×d), independent of the statement, capsules, errors, source secrets and encryption coins. For leaves i=1..n, independently sample S_i uniformly in Z_q^(d×ell), E_i from chi^(m×ell) with ||E_i||_infinity <= beta, then publish B_i=A S_i+E_i mod q. Leaf ORIGINAL witnesses are actual (S_i,E_i) satisfying these exact public equations and bounds. Each leaf has an independent secret even though all have one shared A. No public matrix-expansion seed is disclosed.

Choose a fresh precommitted root T_j for each capsule j. For every seed byte b, choose an independent degree-(k-1) Shamir polynomial over GF(256) with constant term T_{j,b}, and send the evaluation at the distinct nonzero alpha_i to leaf i. For each packed ell-bit share block mu choose new independent r in {-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3}^m and publish u=A^T r, v_i=B_i^T r+floor(q/2) mu modulo q. Each ORIGINAL leaf secret recovers mu via v_i-S_i^T u=E_i^T r+Delta mu. The deterministic decoding margin is 3m beta < q/4-1. Any k different valid ORIGINAL leaf witnesses reconstruct the SAME precommitted T_j via interpolation, then derive P_j||Bcheck_j=PRG(T_j), unmask the SAME preauthorized native signature sigma_j=Dsig_j XOR P_j, and check the bound intended native challenge.

The public joint view includes the restricted relation, claim/UTXO, branch, anchor window, context, native VK, one A, the separate B_i, ALL actual capsules and ciphertexts, manifest, Dsig, checking keys, auxiliary records, audit attestations and related capsules. All ORIGINAL evidence stays off chain. This does not compile arbitrary SYS/BTC witnesses or add NP verification to Bitcoin.

## Why the shared A does not weaken the standard-LWE hypothesis

For an arbitrary QPT adversary with independent quantum advice and a fixed revealed subset U of fewer than k actual source leaves, a straight-line challenger targeting an unopened TRUE leaf i receives an actual ordinary-LWE-PKE IND-CPA challenge public key (A,B_i). It reuses exactly that A for EVERY other leaf, independently samples S_j,E_j for j!=i and computes B_j=A S_j+E_j. This reproduces the real multi-key distribution EXACTLY, including the known secrets/errors of any other revealed leaves. The simulator samples the original capsule roots, coefficients, signatures, unrelated ciphertexts and all other challenge messages itself. It submits the actual target Shamir share versus zero to the IND-CPA challenger. It then computes every manifest, checking key, auxiliary field and related-capsule correlation from the ACTUAL challenge ciphertext bytes and its own known roots/checking secrets. It does not know the challenge secret, challenge coins or use SourceOpen.

The resulting two whole-public-output hybrids are perfectly simulated, not merely their marginal ciphertext. One adjacent hybrid costs the ordinary QPT-IND-CPA advantage of ONE packed group, with no extra factor arising solely from shared A. A total of G unopened independent groups across all relevant capsules costs at most G epsilon_PKE. Quantum-coherent processing of this classical transcript is permitted; the reduction never rewinds. For a classically adaptively disclosed fixed-constant number of leaves, only R93's preguessed-target-set bound applies, not an arbitrary selective-opening guarantee.

After zero-hybrid replacement, R92's Vandermonde proof implies fewer than k opened shares jointly contain no information on T. In the conditioned PRG hybrid, recover-a-candidate-seed testing uses R93's corrected generator-image terminal 2^(kappa-L), not an unjustified 2^-kappa PRG challenge detector. Accepted unauthorized native signatures reduce separately to independently QPT-EUF-CMA native unforgeability, with a uniformly masked signature and no target signing-oracle request in the final uniform-pad hybrid. These are conditional reductions requiring ordinary QPT-LWE PKE, independently QPT-secure PRG and independently QPT-secure native signatures. They are not practical parameter/security estimates.

For a sampled FALSE leaf, B_i is uniform conditional on A. For q prime >6 and distinct r,r' in the septenary cube, (A^T(r-r'), B_i^T(r-r')) is uniform over Z_q^(d+ell); hence its strong leftover-hash statistical error is <= (1/2) sqrt(q^(d+ell)/7^m) per independently encrypted group. Other leaf records are channels of the already public A and their own independent randomness, so they do not increase that group's distance. Count every group across related capsules, giving G_false epsilon_LHL. The probability that a uniform B_i accidentally has SOME bounded-error LWE secret is at most q^(d ell) ((2 beta+1)/q)^(m ell) by a union bound (all parameters must make the bound nonvacuous). This is sampled-FALSE security, NOT arbitrary fixed-FALSE statement security.

The assumption is honest uniform A and independently honest leaf S_i/E_i conditional on A. The argument does NOT cover malicious leaf B values correlated with a target B, maliciously biased A, corrupting the target secret, cross-claim reuse of a leaf secret, target-correlated quantum advice, superposition opening oracles, or malformed ceremony records. Reusing the PUBLIC A across unrelated scopes is allowed if their SECRET S_i/E_i and keys remain independent; it is not R94's reuse of the SAME secret leaf key across unrelated claims. Malicious N-of-N aborts and the graph/DA/Bitcoin endpoint remain conditional.

A public seed a with A=G(a) is NOT an alternative justified by this theorem: publishing a alongside a claimed uniform A makes the correlation publicly testable. R100 does not use a seeded matrix or assume seeded-LWE security.
