## Exact distributions and classical PPT algorithms

Let q be an odd prime, n,m,N,J,L polynomial in security parameter lambda, N>=2, chi an efficiently sampled bounded error distribution with support [-B,B], and g=floor(q/2). Require q>4NmB+2. Draw A uniformly in Z_q^(n by m), s uniformly in Z_q^n, e from chi^m, b=A^T s+e mod q. Publish SOURCE H=[A;b^T]. Any (s',e') with b=A^T s'+e', ||e'||_infty<=B is a valid SOURCE witness. There is no SourceOpen oracle.

The genuine native signer KeyGen creates (sk_N,pk_N) independently of H. Encode its COMPLETE usable sk_N as L bits a_ell. For each bit choose z_(1,ell),...,z_(N-1,ell) independently uniformly in Z_q, and set z_(N,ell)=a_ell-sum_(i<N)z_(i,ell) mod q. Every set of N−1 shares is jointly uniformly distributed and independent of sk_N,pk_N. Shares go to N contributors via private authenticated channels; one protected contributor h must keep its share and randomness secret. An honest key-generating dealer or independently proved MPC is required to establish correct sharing and real checking-key correlation. The dealer-assisted honest-erasure baseline is defined below; this is not malicious-secure distributed KeyGen.

For capsule j in {1,...,J}, each contributor i and bit ell independently samples r_(j,i,ell) uniformly in {0,1}^m and publishes its complete ciphertext:
    u_(j,i,ell)=A*r_(j,i,ell),
    v_(j,i,ell)=b^T*r_(j,i,ell)+g*z_(i,ell) mod q.
The separate assembler receives only H, these encrypted contributions, independent contributor authentication and canonical contextual data. It computes and publishes compact
    C_(j,ell)=(sum_i u_(j,i,ell), sum_i v_(j,i,ell)) mod q.
Full authenticated individual contribution transcripts may remain off chain for audit. Builder coin retention is allowed for builder-generated data and all corrupt contributor coins; protected h's coins are never delivered to or generated by the builder. The builder never computes sk_N. Disclosing protected h's r would immediately expose its q-ary share through v-b^T r, and is expressly outside the model.

For ANY valid (s',e'), off-chain Release computes:
    v_(j,ell)-s'^T*u_(j,ell)
      = g*sum_i z_(i,ell)+e'^T*sum_i r_(j,i,ell)
      = g*a_ell + noise (mod q).
Since |noise|<=NmB<min(g,q-g)/2, nearest-center decoding to 0 or g recovers a_ell deterministically, for every valid SOURCE witness, including different witness states. Every related capsule releases the SAME precommitted sk_N and checks against the genuine pk_N. Authorized use signs the intended claim/path challenge off-chain with an ordinary native signature. Bitcoin is NEVER asked to run the NP verifier or examine large SOURCE evidence. Native no-EC/P2MR-like PQ signatures remain hypothetical, not deployed functionality.

The capsule binds relation identity R_LWE, claim/UTXO, branch, intended native pk_N, challenge path, anchors/window/context and source H. SAME native sk_N may be used in multiple independently encrypted capsules ONLY if they are aliases of the SAME authorization scope. Reusing sk_N for another claim/path would let a legitimate release of one authorize the others; this is not a permitted related-capsule mode.

## Exact full-public QPT experiment and reduction

Assume independently (1) nonuniform QPT decisional LWE distinguishing (A,A^T s+e) from uniform (A,b) for the specified q,n,m,chi, and (2) nonuniform QPT native EUF-CMA with classical signing queries for genuine pk_N. Neither assumption is asserted to have calibrated 128-bit security here. A fixed coalition C of at most N−1 corrupted contributors is chosen before sharing, and at least one protected h remains. Its full-public view includes H, genuine pk_N, every contributor ciphertext and aggregate, source/claim/context and independent contribution authenticators, public/simulatable aux data, J related SAME-key capsules, the entire N−1 corrupted private shares and encryption coins disclosed adaptively, and any classical nonchallenge native signatures. Quantum advice may be arbitrary nonuniform but must have no hidden honest-share/native-secret or SOURCE-witness correlation beyond genuine pk_N and corrupted shares. QPT adversaries may process the classical transcript coherently; no quantum superposition signing/opening access is promised. Public share-to-pk proofs, source-witness leaks, protected h coins/shares, malicious dealer coins and unrelated scope reuse are excluded unless separately simulated.

For uniform H in Z_q^((n+1) by m), distinct binary r,r' differ in a coordinate ±1, a unit modulo q. Thus H*r is a pairwise-universal binary-input hash family. The exact collision second moment and Cauchy-Schwarz yield:
    delta=E_H SD(H*r,Uniform(Z_q^(n+1)))
         <=(1/2)*sqrt((q^(n+1)-1)/2^m).
This is an averaged whole-(u,v) ciphertext bound, not a statement about every fixed H. For any q-ary z, encryption (A*r,b^T*r+g*z) differs only by a translation of the final coordinate, so encryptions of ANY z vs zero have average distance <=2delta. Even conditioned on the real checking key, all N−1 corrupted shares/coins and any independent auxiliary data, protected z_h may be any function of genuine native sk_N, pk_N and those shares, but is independent of H. For J*L independently randomized protected ciphertexts sharing the SAME H and pk_N, a product hybrid costs <=2JLdelta. Public aggregation, authenticators generated from independent authentication keys, adaptive QPT processing and disclosure of corrupt coins cannot increase distinguishing advantage.

Four games, all straight-line, all public correlations generated:
G0: honest TRUE LWE-source H; genuine native KeyGen; q-ary native-key sharing; all real contributor ciphertexts; same-key related capsules and allowed corrupted-coin/secret openings.
G1: replace H with uniform, keeping every other source-dependent output generated faithfully; <=Adv_QPT-DecLWE.
G2: replace ONLY the protected contributor's J*L ciphertexts by independent encryptions of zero under uniform H; preserve actual pk_N, all corrupted plaintext shares/coins/ciphertexts, context and independently generated authenticator data; <=2JLdelta.
G3: replace uniform H with TRUE-LWE H while retaining protected-zero ciphertexts; <=Adv_QPT-DecLWE. A native EUF-CMA challenger hands the simulator only genuine pk_N and a classical signing oracle, NOT sk_N. The simulator samples N−1 corrupted q-ary share vectors uniformly independently of pk_N (their exact real distribution), generates all their coins and ciphertexts, emits protected zero ciphertexts, and simulates J related scopes sharing the same real pk_N. It answers adaptive corrupt-share/coin openings exactly. It needs no SourceOpen oracle, planted bypass secret, witness supplied by the attacker, quantum rewinding, random-oracle extraction or idealized oracle.

If a QPT adversary outputs a usable target native secret, the simulator signs an unqueried intended challenge message; a fresh valid native signature is already a native forgery. Hence under the stated TRUE-source distribution, one target scope and predeclared corrupted cohort:
    Pr[unauthorized target native-key recovery or fresh native forgery]
      <=2 Adv_QPT-DecLWE + 2 J L delta + Adv_QPT-native-EUF-CMA.
All public checking-key/share correlations are preserved. The bounds do NOT establish every-fixed-FALSE hiding or an arbitrary hard-TRUE ORIGINAL-to-witness extractor. The theorem assumes honest input share generation, a nonleaking protected contributor, and no hidden auxiliary source witness.

## Fixed-FALSE counterexample; honest-erasure setup boundary

Every-fixed-FALSE SOURCE security still fails. At q=257,B=0,A=0,b=(1,0,...,0), SOURCE has no witness, but an honest contributor's v=g*z_h+r_0. Given the other N−1 q-ary shares, possible z_h values for native bit 0 and 1 differ by 1, producing disjoint supports {g*z_h,g*z_h+1} and {g*(z_h+1),g*(z_h+1)+1} modulo q. The target bit is public. The theorem deliberately restricts to **honestly sampled TRUE LWE** sources, not arbitrary fixed x.

One concrete limited setup has a separate **honest-erasure dealer** perform genuine native KeyGen, privately distribute q-ary shares, check and authenticate received contributor ciphertexts before funding, then erase complete sk_N, all full share lists and verification coins. The untrusted assembler never holds native sk_N and can retain every assembler coin. This is not a dealerless or malicious-secure N-of-N ceremony. A malicious dealer can retain sk_N, inject bypass or malformed share commitments, or grief availability; public proof that encrypted shares reconstruct the actual pk_N would need a separately instantiated QPT-simulatable ceremony. N-of-N preauthorization with one honest veto/erasure, graph edges, abort security, pre-signature DA and propagation rules remain independent obligations. Valid evidence can only recover after it is available off-chain; no Bitcoin SOURCE verifier, committee or release authority is introduced.
