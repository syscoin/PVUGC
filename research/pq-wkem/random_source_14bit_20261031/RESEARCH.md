## Exact unconditional all-witness correctness lemma

For any **fixed** binary w, the random event Aw=t has probability q^{-d}, independent of all encryption errors. Independently, chi=U(-B,...,B) has mean 0, variance sigma²=B(B+1)/3, and absolute bound B. Thus S_w is a sum of (1+|w|_1) independent centered bounded terms, Var(S_w)<=V=(n+1)*sigma². Elementary Bernstein gives Pr[|S_w|>=u] <= 2 exp(-u²/[2(V+B*u/3)]), with integer failure threshold u=ceil(g/2). Sum over **all 2^n binary w and all T ciphertexts** to get

  eta := Pr[exists valid w and any failing block] <= T*2^n*q^{-d}*2 exp(-u²/[2(V+B*u/3)]).

No independence between witnesses or capsules is required for this union bound. Distinct binary w != w' have pairwise independent events Aw=t and Aw'=t for uniformly generated A,t over odd prime q. Therefore, for N=#binary witnesses, E[N]=2^n/q^d and Var(N)=E[N]*(1-q^{-d}), yielding by the second moment inequality

  delta := Pr[N=0] <= (q^d-1)/(2^n+q^d-1) < q^d/2^n.

Hence **one simultaneous event** has Pr[any valid witness misdecodes any capsule | TRUE] <= eta/(1-delta). For m shared-A independent target scopes, add a union bound of m eta and m delta. This is negligible *probability over honest random SOURCE and LWE coins*, not a polynomial-time certificate that an individual output is error-free.
