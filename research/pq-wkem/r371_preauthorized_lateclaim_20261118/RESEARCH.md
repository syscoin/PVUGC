## Actual new technical move, not the missing secret-release advance
R370 noted that one-time N-of-N participants at t0 cannot pre-sign a cancellation or initiation digest containing an unknown future claim and outpoint, seemingly requiring a protected FE InitOpen in addition to CancelOpen. I tested a narrower native Bitcoin alternative for INITIATION only.

At t0, freeze one vault funding outpoint u0, its value V, transaction version, sequence, locktime, and the entire P2WSH vault input scriptCode. Each of the N setup participants has a distinct INIT signing key alpha_i independent of its CANCEL signing key beta_i. The vault initiation script requires both (i) all N init signatures and (ii) 1-of-K permitted notary signatures, in the same input. The separate activation output script requires all N cancel signatures for early safe cancellation and has a delayed owner settlement path. No alternate subset-controlled early cancellation edge is permitted.

Choose one exact activation output o* = (V, P2WSH(script_cancel)), committing the entire funded vault value. At t0 the participants publish N signatures over a skeleton with input0=u0, output0=o*, using SegWit-v0 SIGHASH_SINGLE|ANYONECANPAY (0x83). For an input i this BIP143 digest commits:
    SHA256d(version || 0^256 || 0^256 || prevout_i ||
            varbytes(scriptCode) || amount_i || sequence_i ||
            SHA256d(serialize(output_i)) || locktime || 0x83000000).
Its 0^256 prevouts/sequence commitments are those of ANYONECANPAY and SINGLE. No other inputs or outputs enter the digest. For a future t1 claim W, one authorized NOTARY constructs activation A_W using the same input0/output0 but appends a zero-value OP_RETURN H(sid||W_body) output and sponsor input(s) plus sponsor change for ALL mining fees. That notary supplies its own SIGHASH_ALL signature, covering the complete claim-bearing transaction. The prepublished N 0x83 signatures remain valid. One notary, not N setup participants, is online at t1. The source proof is never put on Bitcoin.

Changing W_body changes activation txid, hence cancellation outpoint u_W=(txid(A_W),0), assuming hash binding. The cancellation signatures MUST use ordinary SIGHASH_ALL over this actual outpoint and the safe destination. No old released cancellation signature transfers to another claim's different outpoint; this condition is distinct from the earlier R225 common-fixed-outpoint failure. A future source-gated CancelOpen must still verify the actual signed activation, claim, source baseline, all three ORIGINAL OR challenge branches, exact outpoint, amount, native message, window/deadline/reset and safe destination. It must work for any ORIGINAL witness and be public/offline with full joint security. NONE of that FE release was implemented.

## Security qualifications and proof dependency
The entire V must go to the fixed activation output; sponsor funds must pay all transaction fees (or a preapproved fixed limited deduction must be covered at t0). The funding txid must be fixed before all setup signatures are issued and funding must not be irreversibly broadcast until the graph, signatures, source policy, and all needed capsules are verified. The vault script must require the one-notary signature IN ADDITION TO all N published setup signatures: otherwise anyone with the public N signatures initiates. The notary signature may contain a different sighash flag only if the exact full-transaction authorization policy is preserved; the tested design uses SIGHASH_ALL. Native Bitcoin standardness, stack order, sigops, reorg/censorship and CSV challenge-window sizing still require separate node-level validation.

More importantly, this template does NOT cryptographically ensure AllowedWithdrawal(W) at t1. A malicious permitted notary can sign an invalid withdrawal despite preserving the fixed protected output. To meet the ORIGINAL threat model, invalid activation must have a genuinely finite, authenticated, timely claim-specific burn/authorization fault witness and safe payout timing; otherwise a separate enforceable eligibility gate, potentially the missing InitOpen, is still required. An absent ordinary Merkle path or withheld DA is not a no-burn proof. A notary signature proves authorization by that key, NOT truthful burn/source eligibility. Timeout owner recovery must not cause unsafe settlement before admissible evidence can veto, and cannot substitute for a proof of no future fork.

A single fixed vault outpoint can confirm at most one competing initiation, but public pre-signatures may authenticate many mutually exclusive unconfirmed candidate activations. The public FE cancel program must not release against invented, unconfirmed, misindexed, or irrelevant candidates. Sibling capsules, malicious source coins, key erasure, native-VK correlations and selective openings remain essential.
