## Scoped theorem
An n-bit reversible circuit P containing arbitrary affine gates and r Toffoli gates has the exact representation P(s)=A*s XOR b XOR SUM_{j<=r} v_j*h_j(s), where A is invertible, v_j are fixed output vectors and h_j are arbitrary Boolean scalar functions. Let W=span(v_j), dim(W)<=r.
Run528's legitimate selectable public witness writers, started at the bound anchor P(K_x,x,0,0,marker), produce in k steps for each chosen k-bit w the output Y_w=P(K_x,x,w,k,marker). Since s_w=s_0+Lw, with L the injective witness-slot embedding, all Y_w belong to one affine subspace P_aff(s_0)+Im(A*L)+W of dimension <=k+r. Hence for q distinct selected witnesses: rank_F2{Y_wi XOR Y_w0}_{i=1..q-1}<=k+r.
When q=k+r+2<=min(2^k,n+1), sparse-frame maximal rank q-1 is impossible. The attack needs only k*q sanctioned forward writer calls, plus classical GF(2) elimination. It does not need frame inverse, global translation, arbitrary encoded-state probes, open references or native checking keys.
For a uniform hidden permutation, the q distinct semantic inputs have uniform distinct n-bit output labels. Writing N=2^n, the exact full-affine-rank probability is PROD_{i=1}^{q-1} (N-2^(i-1))/(N-i): after i affinely independent labels, their affine span has 2^(i-1) points and the next one is selected among N-i unused labels. This supplies the exact one-sided distinguishing advantage against the ideal permutation benchmark.
The comparison is between sparse P and an ideal exponential-size random P. The result does NOT recover the false-instance seed, forge a native signature, extract ORIGINAL witnesses or refute general nonlinear local-mixing/RIO. Pseudorandom honest-path labels are a stronger requirement than WE-like key hiding. Compared with Run529, only ordinary chosen witness-path outputs are necessary; unlike Run529's derived-translation approach, no reversible paired displacement is used. Preconditions q<=2^k and q<=n+1 matter.
