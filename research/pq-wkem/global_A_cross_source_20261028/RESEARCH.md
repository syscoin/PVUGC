## Construction and distributions

Let q be an odd prime, m>n, d,t>=1, 2h<q, g=2h+1, radix T=floor(q/g)>=2, capability length kappa, ell=ceil(log_T(2^kappa)), and L=ceil(ell/d). Setup samples ONE public A uniformly from F_q^(m x n). For each of M distinct original bounded-matrix-LWE statements j, the TRUE security experiment independently samples S_j uniform F_q^(n x d), E_j uniform {-1,0,1}^(m x d), and sets B_j=A S_j+E_j modulo q. These choices are mutually independent CONDITIONAL ON A, not unconditionally independent. Public Build receives (A,B_j), NOT S_j/E_j. Its restricted ORIGINAL relation checks W_j=(S,E), B_j=A S+E, ternary E, and the typed claim/UTXO/branch/anchors/window/context and intended native checking key. This is an actual bounded-matrix-LWE relation, not a generic burn or authorization NP relation.

For each j, Build creates an independent honest native signing seed K_j and its genuine pk(K_j), and independent uniform kappa-bit U_j and t XOR shares Z_{j,i}. Public D_j=U_j XOR (XOR_i Z_{j,i}), V_j=K_j XOR U_j. For every share and batch, independently sample signed r in the integer L1 sphere S(m,h) of norm h and publish

    c=A^T r mod q,
    v=B_j^T r + g a mod q,

where a is a d-vector of radix-T digits, padded with zeros in the last batch. Full public output comprises A, every claim-specific B_j, all claim/relation/context bindings, real checking keys, D/V, ciphertexts, permitted auxiliaries, and related capsules. Real native keys and share/capsule coins are independently generated between different claims. A is the ONLY common cryptographic source object. A valid SOURCE witness for claim j must NOT be usable as an authorization credential for an unrelated claim; hence NEVER reuse its B_j and K_j across independent claims. Context labels alone do not revoke the algebraic opening of a reused B.

## Exact all-ORIGINAL-witness correctness for the restricted relation

For EVERY valid W_j=(S,E), off-chain Release checks the complete ORIGINAL source relation and computes

    v - S^T c = g a + E^T r (mod q).

Every centered error coordinate is at most norm_1(r)=h; all legitimate cyclic radix centers are separated by at least g=2h+1, including the wraparound gap since g*T<=q. Therefore nearest cyclic decoding yields every original a, every Z_{j,i}, and the ONE SAME precommitted K_j for any valid W_j. No canonical witness or common intermediate state is needed for this correctness statement. The illustrative planted distribution is not claimed to exhibit many distinct source witnesses.

## Full joint-public QPT security of an uncompromised selected target

All setup/build/release algorithms are classical PPT. The adversary and reductions are arbitrary QPT with efficiently preparable, simulation-compatible quantum advice, classical signing/opening queries, and no superposition oracle. Fix a target claim j*. Other source matrices B_k and source witnesses (S_k,E_k) are honestly independently sampled given A, and may ALL be fully disclosed to the adversary along with their entire native signing keys, share plaintexts, and all encryption coins. For target j*, at most t-1 WHOLE shares, each with all their coins, may be adaptively opened; no target source witness, native seed, or hidden-share coins are leaked. Auxiliary data must be efficiently simulatable from public sources, public target pk, claims/contexts, allowed classical signing transcripts, disclosed non-target secrets, permitted target openings and challenge-independent advice. Malicious correlated other B sources, arbitrary partial opening of each share, arbitrary secret-dependent leakage, malformed setup, and quantum signing/opening oracles are outside this theorem.

1. **Source DecLWE hybrid:** Given one real-or-uniform scalar DecLWE challenge (A,b), embed b as one target B_{j*} column. Generate every other target column in its ordinary planted/uniform hybrid and every non-target B_k=A S_k+E_k using known independently sampled witnesses. All non-target source witnesses, complete share coins, keys, and disclosures are exactly simulatable from public A. Generate genuine target pk(K), XOR correlations D,V, public context, source ciphertexts and permitted target openings. No target SOURCE opening, SourceOpen oracle, adversary-supplied representation, or quantum rewinding is invoked. A d-column hybrid bounds the complete-view TRUE-versus-uniform target source distinguishing advantage by d * Adv_QPT-DecLWE, with no additional factor M for a target selected in advance.

2. **Uniform target full-ciphertext hybrid:** At its uniform stage H_*=[A B_{j*}] is uniform F_q^(m x(n+d)). Non-target source data and disclosed non-target witnesses are independent of B_{j*} and target coins given A. For two different signed sphere vectors r,r', the nonzero difference modulo q has uniform H_*^T difference; pair collision probability averaged over the genuine joint A,B_{j*} distribution is exactly q^(-(n+d)). With N(m,h)=sum_{k=1}^{min(m,h)} 2^k C(m,k) C(h-1,k-1), the expected conditional ciphertext-vs-uniform statistical distance is at most delta_d=0.5 sqrt((q^(n+d)-1)/N(m,h)). Replace L independent ciphertext vectors for ONE chosen unopened target share at total cost L delta_d. This is a statement averaged over random A and target B; NOT a conditional guarantee for every fixed or maliciously selected A. Giving non-target source witnesses and coins only applies a simulatable channel conditioned on A; it does not invalidate the joint distribution bound. Coherent QPT postprocessing cannot increase this classical-input trace/statistical distance.

3. **Adaptive full-coin survivor and native check:** Uniformly guess survivor share i* among t; score a target key recovery/fresh intended-path signature only if i* stays unopened. Every successful valid transcript with at most t-1 whole target-share openings leaves at least one survivor, giving a factor at most t. All others are honestly encrypted and their disclosed r and Z replay exactly; opening i* aborts the counted event. The final native QPT EUF-CMA simulator receives real pk(K_*) without K_*, samples D_* and V_* uniformly, keeps other target shares/coins genuine, and replaces unopened survivor ciphertexts by independent uniform ones. For fixed K_* and all other Z, (U_*,Z_survivor) maps bijectively to (V_*,D_*); thus the simulation retains genuine checking-key, XOR, source and cross-claim public correlations. If the adversary outputs usable native K_* it can sign a fresh intended-path message, otherwise an accepted fresh signature already wins EUF-CMA. Hence:

    Pr[unauthorized selected-target K recovery OR fresh intended-path native forgery]
       <= t (d Adv_QPT-DecLWE + L delta_d + Adv_QPT-native-EUF-CMA).

For M simultaneously uncompromised target choices, a conservative union/target-guessing bound multiplies by M; disclosing a target's own source witness or all its whole shares makes that target legitimately openable and removes it from the unauthorized game. This result has NO fixed-false arbitrary-NP security and NO generic hard-true key recovery -> ORIGINAL NP witness extraction. Standard QPT decisional-LWE and native QPT EUF-CMA are the independent assumptions; the numeric parameters below are not certified for either.
