## Full-public sparse-support reconstruction lemma

Let A=supp(P) union {empty monomial}. For every m in A and every t in supp(f_j) with t subset m, enumerate all u with u union t=m. Each is (m minus t) union v for a subset v of t: at most 2^d choices per (j,m,t). Put G={u f_j : all such enumerations, u f_j !=0}. Thus |G| <= (|supp(P)|+1) sum_j L_j 2^d = poly(lambda) for bounded-arity constraints and a polynomially sparse published P, independent of hidden multiplier degree.

For any actual hidden nonzero multiplier term (j,u), choose one monomial m in supp(u f_j). The coefficient P_m contains its independent uniform b_(j,u), XOR other independent terms and at most the frozen S for m=empty; hence Pr[P_m=0]=2^-lambda. When P_m !=0 the enumeration necessarily includes (j,u). By union bound, with probability >=1-M*2^-lambda all M nonzero hidden multiplier supports are included in PUBLIC G.
