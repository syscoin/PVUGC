## Selection before implementation

Exact missing operation: given the complete bounded original claim relation R(x,w), publish a capsule that opens one frozen cancellation contribution S for every future qualifying original certificate, with no source witness available to Build in general. All three OR grounds, contextual admission, maturity, relevance and native binding remain required.

Closest attack R289: locally available labels from incompatible source traces can be combined at an accepting gate. Mathematical change considered: replace the overlapping circuit with a **decomposable guarded formula** in which every conjunction combines disjoint sets of source variables, not just independently randomized labels. An OR selects a single complete alternative; an AND combines partial assignments with disjoint supports. This is a semantic condition, rather than a MAC or another hidden coefficient basis. Investigate an explicit recursive capsule over such a formula, and whether the original source gates admit independently constructible hash pads. This would remove the specific inconsistent assignment combination if the compiler and atom release actually existed. The latter must not be assumed.

## Serialized recursive candidate

Let F be a tree of AND/OR gates with predicates P_i(x,w restricted to V_i) at leaves, with the union of variables of each AND child pairwise disjoint. The predicates may be computationally hard to satisfy. F must be equivalent to the *original* R, with efficiently decodable actual certificate assignments. A syntactic duplicate of a source variable is not a disjoint source variable unless decoding imposes and verifies its equality; that equality itself couples the two supports.

For a node v with incoming uniform lambda-bit key k_v, at an OR send k_v to every child; at an AND of t children choose t-1 independent uniform strings and set the remaining child key to k_v XOR the first t-1. Root key k is independent of S; publish S XOR a context-separated hash stream of k. For each leaf i with a finite list of actual admissible source openings a_i,j known to Build, publish
    c_i,j = k_i XOR H("guard-pad", ctx, occurrence_i, canonical(a_i,j)).
The list must be a complete cover of every satisfying local assignment if this is to provide every-witness completeness. The row need not carry the clear opening: a row index can be H("guard-index",ctx,occurrence_i,canonical(a_i,j)), in a distinct domain, and lookup must be unambiguous. Open uses the local portion of w to select and decrypt its leaf row, XORs all child keys for AND, and selects one qualifying branch for OR. Induction gives exact root recovery whenever every needed leaf has its row.

The structural consistency property is precise. If an accepting subtree is assembled from local satisfying leaf assignments, choose just one child at each OR, and at each AND union the child assignments. Their supports are disjoint by hypothesis, so no equality between two inconsistent choices can be violated; extend unspecified input bits arbitrarily. Recursing gives an actual global satisfying assignment. This does not establish that an arbitrary public recovery adversary supplies any such assignments, and is not a QPT extraction theorem. It only explains the mathematical change relative to the R289 splice; the previous overlapping local labels do not have this property.

For a purely structural erasure experiment in which unrecovered leaf rows are independent uniform strings (not a justified hybrid for the actual hash capsule), a false subtree hides its incoming key: AND has one false child masking the XOR, OR has every child false and hence every independent subencoding hides the shared key. The claim is conditional on genuine independent row erasures and fresh randomness at each occurrence, and is not a joint simulation of actual correlated source openings, row indices, native VK, auxiliary information or siblings. A full public QROM argument would have to handle those correlations and extract actual openings without requiring an adversary's trace or representation. This experiment is mathematical only, not executed.

## Attempted original instantiation and selection rejection

There are two distinct ways to choose atoms.

A. All leaf predicates are explicit public single-bit literals or finite allowed-value tables, and F is an explicit polynomial decomposable formula. Then the proposed representation itself supplies a polynomial certificate search procedure: mark each leaf satisfiable by scanning its explicit allowed values; OR is satisfiable if a child is; AND is satisfiable if every child is. Record a choice at each OR and local values at leaves, and use the disjoint-support induction to obtain a complete assignment. Thus an efficient public compiler of this form plus decoder cannot preserve a distribution in which original certificates are computationally hard to find, even without reading any capsule rows. If every actual original instance happened to be easy, that would not establish the requested hard-TRUE protection. This is a conditional statement about the specified explicit representation, not an impossibility of all succinct or hidden encodings. Adding equality checks after the union loses the claimed structural property; compiling them into the formula must pay the cost of the coupling.

B. Retain large opaque atomic predicates, for example one for the exact qualified competing BTC history from the bound anchor, or one for a complete original Syscoin incompatible-history certificate. This avoids the explicit satisfiability search only by retaining hard leaf operations. Their rows are not available to Build. For the BTC branch, future qualifying histories may have different complete canonical header bytes, hashes, targets and work sums; only their admission and context are fixed. The fixed claim anchor is public and cannot be a secret pad. A setup-known prior header is not a substitute for the future qualifying history. For the burn and Syscoin branches the same capsule must accept original evidence that varies across later states; it cannot restrict to a preselected local opening. Do not assume that a particular authenticated nonmembership path exists or that a malformed wrapper was admitted. No new Core facts are claimed.

Replacing the enumerated guard rows by one common pad indexed by the public verdict P_i=1 allows anyone to recover the leaf key from that verdict without satisfying the guard. Replacing them by one pad derived from a setup-known source opening fails pointwise completeness on different valid openings (in an ideal hash model the independently addressed pads agree only with probability 2^-lambda for a block). Publishing both input label choices reintroduces source-independent leaf traversal. A public guard evaluator that verifies the original input then returns the hidden k_i is precisely the missing serialized operation. These are the already-known public-verdict/source-pad attacks unchanged at the leaf, so reject B before implementing or enlarging a fixture. No claim that every conceivable choice of hard atom fails; no construction of a valid original atom was found.

In particular, flattening the top-level three OR grounds into three opaque leaves is not progress. Keeping just the easy atoms cannot preserve the independently sufficient original branches. A public formula over the bits of a source certificate can be compiled by expanding accepting assignments, but the table may be exponential and Build would have to enumerate them. A shared DAG optimization would require a new analysis of repeated child keys and contexts; it does not repair either A or B.

## Handoff

The selection trial identifies where decomposition would genuinely eliminate the particular cross-assignment splice, and why it does not provide a source release: either the public finite-domain representation itself finds a certificate, or an opaque source atom still needs a hash-only public ciphertext generated before its unknown future opening. Do not dedicate another run to treewidth, DNNF packing, larger tables, rearranging variable copies, or testing independent leaf pads. A viable next mathematical change must implement the hard atom operation from actual original authentication, without giving public verdict access or assuming a common preimage known at Build. It must then establish arbitrary-QPT full-public security, not merely the extraction of a volunteered accepting subtree. No standalone restricted result qualifies.
